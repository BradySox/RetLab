* **[Campaigns]** **New campaign: Kola - Northern Flank 1985.** The Soviets hold Finnmark and push down the one coast road toward Bardufoss while the Northern Fleet leaves Murmansk. Stock DCS units only, with two new factions: `NATO Northern Flank 1985` and `USSR Northern Fleet 1985`. Built from Starfire's *Able Archer 83*, which still ships unchanged.
