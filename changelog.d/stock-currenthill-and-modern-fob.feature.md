* **[Factions]** The base game's CurrentHill units join the modern factions: the Tiger UHT attack helicopter, BMP-3 ERA, Titan, M1296 Dragoon, and the HX77, HX81 and SLT-50 trucks. No mod needed.
* **[Mission Generation]** Factions from 2000 on build their FOBs inside a HESCO wall with watchtowers, tents and a gated entrance, and put a few tents and props beside the FOB's helipads.
