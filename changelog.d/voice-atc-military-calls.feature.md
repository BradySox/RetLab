* **[Mission Generation]** Voice ATC uses military calls. Ground gives the altimeter with taxi; Tower gives the wind with takeoff and approves the frequency change, so no airborne call is needed. Inbound gets "report initial" (or a straight-in to a 3-mile final), and the landing clearance comes at base or final with the gear down. A formation is answered as "<callsign> flight"; "have the numbers" leaves out the altimeter and wind. The ATC card and F10 menu show the new calls.
