* **[Campaign Management]** HQ requests: each turn HQ asks the players to take out up to three enemy targets the auto-planner put no package on, one easy, one mid and one hard to reach. Each stays open for 2 to 5 turns. They are listed under HQ REQUESTS on the map's status bar, ringed in gold on the map, marked in the target panel and on the kneeboard of a flight sent against one, and the SITREP notes the ones destroyed. A request pays nothing. On for new games; older saves keep it off.
