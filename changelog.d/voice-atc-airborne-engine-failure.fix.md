* **[Mission Generation]** Voice ATC: "airborne, switching departure" is an airborne call, not a takeoff request, and "engine failure", "flameout" or "fire" without "mayday" is an emergency, not a start request. A Relaxed readback that includes the wind, altimeter or Tower frequency is no longer answered "negative".
* **[Mission Generation]** Voice ATC on Relaxed matches a misheard callsign as Casual does: the right numbers, the only flight at the field with that name ("Enfield 2" for Enfield 4-1), or the only player flight there. "Clear for takeoff 23" (clear, not cleared) is a readback, and "request runway in use" gets the runway and wind instead of "say again".
