### B271 — Voice phrasing: natural calls and the new bot lines · §117, §119 · ☐ UNTESTED

Needs `BradySox/skyeye` PR #10. Fly a player package with an ABCCC, Voice AWACS and Voice ATC
on, Strict, with a DCS player name that is not the flight callsign.

**Pass:** "Ford 9, cleared for takeoff, runway 28L" (callsign first) gets no second clearance
and no say again. "Ford 9, two ship, ready to taxi" clears ship 1. "Tower, Ford 9, outbound"
gets "frequency change approved". A taxi clearance left unanswered gets one "read back" about
20 s later. "Blackjack, Ford 9-1, ready" after the roll call gets "copy ready" and does not
push the package early. The ABCCC says "Ford package, Blackjack, push" and "push time zero
five one zero" (digits as words). A garbled call to Blackjack gets "Ford niner one,
Blackjack, say again" in Blackjack's voice, not the AWACS's "Last caller". "Image, Ford 9,
bogey dope" with the player named "Flash" gets a BRAA, not "negative radar contact". The
sunrise names the mission's AWACS callsign, not Chainsaw.

**Fail signature:** a callsign-first readback draws "Station calling Tower, say again your
callsign" or a repeated clearance; the ABCCC still says "Package Ford" or "zero five ten";
bogey dope still answers "negative radar contact"; the sunrise says "GCI Chainsaw".
