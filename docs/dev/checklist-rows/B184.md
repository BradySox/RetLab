### B184 — The OPFOR buy/sell/transfer cheat manages red's ground forces · §56 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** Settings → Cheats → tick Enable OPFOR Buy/Sell/Transfer Cheat. Open a red base
that can deploy ground units: it has a Ground Forces HQ tab; buy a tank, sell one, and
order a transfer to another red base. The transfer shows in the transfer list, and a
transfer to a blue base or with no road is refused before anything is taken from the
origin. Untick the cheat: the tab and the red transfer rows go away.

**Fail signature:** no Ground Forces HQ tab with the cheat on; the sell button stays dead;
a refused transfer still removes units from the origin; or red rows stay visible with the
cheat off.
