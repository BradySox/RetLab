### B341 — An armor group sent to another base drives there over turns · §131 · ☐ UNTESTED

A new game with a friendly armor group that has trucks and towed guns (a ZU-23 or howitzer
battery), and a second friendly base by road. Record Tacview.

**Pass:**
- Its window shows **Move...**. Send half the units to the other base; Preview lists the
  trucks added, the miles and the turns.
- The group splits: the moved units show as a new group with miles to go; the rest stay.
- In the next mission the column spawns on the road where it is and drives toward the base
  (Tacview). Towed guns are not spawned on their own.
- Killing a truck on the road also loses the gun it was towing (debrief).
- On arrival the units are in the base's reserve.
- **Stop here** leaves the survivors as their own group on the spot.

**Fail signature:** no Move button with Ground movement on; the column spawns at its origin
every turn or off the road; guns spawn and fire at the departure point; units arrive twice
or vanish; the source group keeps the moved units too.
