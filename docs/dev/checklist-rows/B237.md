### B237 — Desert Aladeen: The H-3 Front plays from a new game · campaign · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** start a new game of "Iraq - Desert Aladeen - The H-3 Front". Blue owns the three
H-3 strips and the Jordan rear; red owns the other 12 airfields plus FOB Wadi Hauran. The one
front line runs H-3 Main to FOB Wadi Hauran, about 85 mi. Blue jets spawn on the H-3 ramps
with no "no parking" errors; the E-3 and tankers air-start from the Jordan rear. North-west
Iraq shows SAM rings and Scud sites around Tal Afar, Sinjar, Rabia, Hatra and Al-Qaim. Take
the FOB and the front moves to FOB Wadi Hauran to Al-Asad; the Al-Qaim sites are still there.

**Fail signature:** an aircraft stuck in "waiting for parking" or deleted at H-3; a target
or SAM unit spawned inside a building or a town; no front line, or one from H-3 to Baghdad;
the Al-Qaim SAM or Scud site gone after the FOB is captured; any mod unit on either side.
