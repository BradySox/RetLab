### B343 — A SAM battery packs up, drives, and rejoins the IADS where it stops · §131 · ☐ UNTESTED

A new game with a friendly self-propelled SAM site (SA-6, SA-11 or SA-15) and Ground movement
on. Record Tacview and keep `dcs.log`.

**Pass:**
- The site's window shows **Move...**; every unit is ticked and greyed. Preview gives miles
  and turns.
- While it is on the road: no threat ring on the map, the site is not in the Skynet network
  in `dcs.log`, and in the mission it drives as a column with radars quiet and holds fire.
- On arrival it stands in its normal layout at the new spot, its ring is back on the map,
  and Skynet lists it again, linked to power and comms in range of its new spot.
- An enemy battery seen moving (outside AI, stage 7) shows as unknown on the map until hit.

**Fail signature:** the battery fires or radiates while packed; it never rejoins Skynet after
arriving; it is still linked to the power station back where it started; its ring shows at
the old spot; units spawn stacked or in a column after arrival.
