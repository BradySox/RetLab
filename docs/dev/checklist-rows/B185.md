### B185 — Tanker, AWACS and CAP boxes read apart on the F10 map · §45 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** in any campaign with a blue tanker, an AWACS and a CAP over water, open the F10
map. Tanker boxes are a solid green outline, AWACS boxes a solid amber outline, CAP
stations a thin dashed cyan outline, and none of them is filled. Each label is in its
box's colour on a dark box and readable without zooming in. Where boxes overlap, each
outline can still be followed.

**Fail signature:** a box is still filled (cyan, or red on a CAP station); the solid line
renders as a faint dotted hairline; tanker and AWACS are the same colour; a label is too
small to read at the zoom where the boxes fit on screen.
