### B176 — Fixed SAM sites show their rings from turn one · §3 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** on a new turn with Recon intel fog on, an enemy SA-2, SA-3, SA-5, SA-10/20, S-400,
Patriot or Hawk site you have never attacked shows its threat ring on the map and on a Viper's
HSD (or a Hornet's SA page) with the DTC loaded. An SA-6 or SA-11 site you have not attacked
shows no ring.

**Fail signature:** a fixed site with no ring on the map or in the jet; or a mobile SAM that
shows its ring before it is engaged.
