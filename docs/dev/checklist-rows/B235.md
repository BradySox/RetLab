### B235 — Juan's Skynet fixes behave in a real mission · Skynet return · ☐ UNTESTED

Built 2026-10-16 from juanjux/Skynet-IADS and Skynet 3.3.0
(`retlab-skynet-return-notes.md`, "Fixes from Juan's fork"). The harness covers the logic;
these need DCS.

**Pass:** on a mission generated after the change, with the Skynet debug output on:

- A red P-37, Nebo-SVU or Nebo-U EWR, or a blue FPS-117, shows its NATO name
  (Bar Lock, Box Spring, Tall Rack, FPS-117) in its `ADDED:` line, and a SAM it covers stays dark until a target
  is in range.
- A CurrentHill Patriot (`usa_2020`), Pantsir-S2 or Tor M2K (`russia_2020`) site logs
  `ADDED:` and does not radiate from mission start.
- A site that goes dark for a HARM comes back up afterwards and engages again (its AI was off
  while it hid). In multiplayer, the HARM misses the dark radar.
- With Mobile SHORAD or MERAD shoot-and-scoot ticked, a site that hides from a HARM drives
  to its new spot.

**Fail signature:** `you have added an SAM site that Skynet IADS can not handle` for one of
those sites; a SAM covered only by one of those EWRs is live from mission start; a site that
went dark for a HARM never fires again; a mobile site hiding from a HARM never moves.
