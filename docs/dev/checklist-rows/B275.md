### B275 — Each voice bot shows under its own name in SRS · §116–§119 · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-22):** the `voiceatc`, `pkgcontrol` and `vacuum` plugins put a
`speaker` on every `say` line (the station name: "Hatzerim Ground", the ABCCC callsign,
"Vacuum"). A SkyEye with BradySox/skyeye#14 opens one SRS client per speaker and sends each
line under it. An older SkyEye ignores the field.

**Pass:** with SkyEye built from #14 and Voice AWACS, Voice ATC, Vacuum and package control
on, open the SRS client list after the mission loads.
- It lists "<SkyEye callsign> [BOT]" (no "GCI " prefix), "<field> Ground [BOT]" and
  "<field> Tower [BOT]" for each ATC field, "Vacuum [BOT]" and the ABCCC "<callsign> [BOT]".
- Call Ground for taxi: the SRS overlay's last-transmitter name reads "<field> Ground [BOT]".
  Call Tower: "<field> Tower [BOT]".
- A Vacuum check-in answer reads "Vacuum [BOT]"; an ABCCC check-in answer on STRIKE reads the
  ABCCC callsign; a SkyEye picture call reads "<SkyEye callsign> [BOT]".
- No two bots talk over each other, and SkyEye does not answer its own bots' calls.

**Fail signature:** every call still reads "GCI <callsign> [BOT]"; a bot's client appears but
its calls are silent; SkyEye's log shows it recognizing a Ground or Tower line as a player
call; the SkyEye log line "too many mission speakers" on a campaign with few fields.
