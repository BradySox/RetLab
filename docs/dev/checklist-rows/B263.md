### B263 — Radio discipline changes how the ABCCC, Vacuum and AWACS behave · §121 · ⊘ RETIRED (removed 2026-10-26, DM: dropped)

**2026-10-26 — REMOVED on the DM's call, never flown.** The Radio discipline setting is
deleted (features doc §121): every voice bot accepts every call, so there is no level to
test. What the bots do now is in B261, B268 and the §117 and §118 rows.

**What changed (2026-10-20):** a Radio discipline setting, Strict by default, with Relaxed
and Casual for a squadron that does not fly by the book.

**Pass:** fly one package on each level with an ABCCC and SkyEye build 5 or later. Strict:
the roll call goes out and a silent SEAD player holds the push. Relaxed: the roll call goes
out, nobody is held for staying silent, and "Colt 1-1, ready to push" with no "Anvil" gets an
answer on STRIKE. Casual: no roll call and no egress call; an escort on the ground holds
nothing; a SEAD on the ground still holds; Vacuum calls only the SAM over your route; on
STRIKE the AWACS gives only threat calls, and a picture asked on STRIKE is answered on the
AWACS frequency.

**Fail signature:** a level makes no difference (the setting is not emitted); a pilot-only
call is ignored off Strict (SkyEye older than build 5); merges still on STRIKE in Casual.
