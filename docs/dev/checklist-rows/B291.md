### B291 — ABCCC egress waits for every jet and weapon; player "off target" answered · §117 · ☐ UNTESTED

Needs BradySox/skyeye `retlab/off-target` (the "offtarget" package call). Fly a player package under an ABCCC with an AI strike flight of two or more jets on dumb bombs (Mk-83s), and a second strike flight or player on a JSOW (or other standoff glide weapon) released well outside 10 NM.

**Pass:** as each AI strike flight leaves the target the ABCCC says "<package> package, <ABCCC>, <flight> off target" (flight callsign spoken without the ship number, "Dodge eight"), only once every jet of that flight is past 10 NM and opening. The package call "<package> package, <ABCCC>, all flights off target, egress" comes only after the last bomb and the last JSOW have hit (check the Tacview impact times), and after the SEAD and escort are 10 NM out. A player calling "<ABCCC>, <callsign>, off target" (or "off", "egressing", "off and egressing") gets "<callsign>, <ABCCC>, copy off, cleared egress" at once and no off-target line from the ABCCC for their flight. No "RTB" in the egress call.

**Fail signature:** the egress call while an AI wingman is still on the target or a weapon is still falling (Test 65, 2026-10-26); no egress call at all with every striker home (a lost, landed or left-behind flight still counted); "say again" to an off-target call (old SkyEye); "egressing" heard as an acknowledgement and not answered.

**Also check:** a package whose strikers are all shot down or land ends with "<package> package, <ABCCC>, package complete." An hour after execute with no egress, the same line, never silence.
