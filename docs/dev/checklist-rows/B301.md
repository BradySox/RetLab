### B301 — SkyEye's radio recordings play in Tacview, lined up · §127 · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-29):** with **Record radios for Tacview (SkyEye)** on and SkyEye build with BradySox/skyeye #25 running, the `radiorecord` plugin asks SkyEye to record the AWACS, STRIKE, Vacuum, Ground, Tower and each player flight's frequency. SkyEye writes one WAV per frequency in Documents\Tacview. See `docs/dev/retlab-features.md` §127.

**Pass:** fly a mission with the setting on: radio check the AWACS, taxi with Ground, and say something on your flight frequency. After the mission, Documents\Tacview holds `SkyEye_... .wav` files next to the `.acmi`. Open the `.acmi` with the Auto Load Media add-on on: each call plays when it happened in the replay (within a second). SkyEye never answered anything said on your flight frequency.

**Fail signature:** no WAV files (check `skyeye.log` for "recording radios for Tacview" and "recording radio frequency"); files that play but drift from the replay; SkyEye answering on the flight frequency; a file missing calls SkyEye spoke.
