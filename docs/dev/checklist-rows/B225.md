### B225 — An anti-ship flight turns for home after its last missile · §8 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** any campaign with an AI anti-ship package that carries only anti-ship missiles
(B-52H, Tu-22M3, H-6J, S-3B). Vectron's Claw turn 1 has four B-52Hs on the Kuznetsov group.
Watch it in Tacview.

- The flight fires from its ingress point, about 80 NM out.
- Once the last missile is off, every aircraft turns away and heads for its landing field.
- No aircraft of the flight comes within 40 NM of the ships.
- A flight that still has weapons (a Hornet with Harpoons left, or with Sidewinders) keeps
  flying its route as before.

**Fail signature:** a bomber with empty racks flying on to the target point over the fleet;
a flight that turns for home before it has fired; a flight that orbits at the ingress
point with no weapons.
