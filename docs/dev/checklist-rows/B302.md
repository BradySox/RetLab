### B302 — Approach gives the chart exit and entry, and hands you to Tower · §119 step 2 · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-29):** each Voice ATC field has an Approach frequency that also works departures. Tower's takeoff clearance says "contact Departure"; Approach gives a chart exit going out and a chart entry coming in (Caucasus), or a point 10 NM out elsewhere, and hands arrivals to Tower. See `docs/dev/retlab-features.md` §119.

**Pass:** on a Caucasus field (Kobuleti, Senaki or Kutaisi), take off, call Departure airborne, fly to the exit you are given and report it: "frequency change approved". Come back, call Approach inbound 20 to 30 miles out: you get the runway and an entry point on the field's VAD kneeboard chart that serves that runway, at the chart altitude. At the entry Approach says "contact Tower"; call Tower and get the runway, wind and "report initial" without repeating anything. On a field off the charts (Syria), Approach sends you 10 miles out on your side of the field.

**Fail signature:** Approach names an entry for the other runway, or one on the far side of the field; no "contact Tower" at the entry; Tower answers "say again" after the handoff; the ATC card lists no Approach frequency or no points; a jet's presets miss Approach.
