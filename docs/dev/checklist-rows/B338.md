### B338 — AI CAPs refuel on station and go back to their orbit · §8 · ☐ UNTESTED

A turn with an AI BARCAP on a long station (an hour or more) and a planned tanker that fits
it (boom for an F-15C or F-16C, basket for a Hornet). Turn **AI flights have unlimited fuel**
off so the CAP actually burns down. Record Tacview; run time acceleration if you like.

**Pass:**
- `dcs.log` has a `CAP FUEL| <group> refuelling at ...` line while the CAP has well over 20
  minutes of station left, then `refuel complete; resuming orbit`.
- In Tacview the CAP leaves its racetrack for the tanker, takes fuel, and flies back to the
  same racetrack.
- It goes home at its planned station end, not later.
- With no tanker in the package or theater, the CAP goes home at bingo as before.

**Fail signature:** the CAP sits on station below bingo and runs dry; it never leaves for a
tanker that is up; it leaves the racetrack for good after tanking; it stays past its station
end; a `CAP FUEL| monitor failed` warning.
