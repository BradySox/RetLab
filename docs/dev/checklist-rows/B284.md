### B284 — Carrier and LHA groups sail a supply ship as a third group · naval layouts · ☐ UNTESTED

**Setup:** a new campaign with a carrier and an LHA (any `usn_1985` campaign).

**Pass:**
- The F10 map shows a third group per carrier and LHA, `<carrier> (Supply)`, one ship of the faction's `cargo_ship` type, 1 to 2 NM astern at mission start.
- The carrier and its escorts still steam together into the wind; only the supply ship falls behind.
- `dcs.log` carries no Skynet or Airboss error about the supply group.

**Fail signature:** the supply ship spawns inside the carrier's or escorts' formation and collides; the escorts slow down; the supply group is missing (the slot was dropped); Skynet logs an error for a group with no radar.
