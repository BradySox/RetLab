### B345 — The outside AI moves a red armor group and a red SAM battery · §131, §109 · ☐ UNTESTED

A new game with Ground movement and Outside AI plans red on, and an outside AI (Claude Code)
connected by Developer tools > Copy AI connect link.

**Pass:**
- Ask the AI to move a red armor group toward a front and a red SA-6 or SA-11 site to a new
  spot. `GET /retribution-ai/ground/movement` lists both with miles and turns left.
- The scripted red planner, run without the AI, never moves a red ground unit.
- In the mission the red columns drive their leg; the SAM column holds fire while packed.
- After it arrives, the moved red SAM shows as unknown on your map until you engage it.
- Asking the API to move a blue group is refused with a reason.

**Fail signature:** a red group moves with no AI order; a move that the preview accepted
fails at dispatch; the moved SAM keeps its known ring at the new spot; the API moves a blue
group; a 500 error instead of `{"ok": false, "error": ...}`.
