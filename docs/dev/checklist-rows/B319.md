### B319 — A player escort's route turns for home at the Escort Hold · §8 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** take the player seat in an Escort or SEAD Escort flight of a DEAD or Strike
package. In the cockpit, step through the route: INGRESS, ESCORT HOLD, then the egress NAV
points and SPLIT. No steerpoint sits on the target. The flight still reaches the join on time.

**Fail signature:** a TARGET steerpoint on the defended site after the Escort Hold; the
escort reaching the join late or early against its package after this change.
