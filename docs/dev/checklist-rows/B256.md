### B256 — Randomize weather fills the form and keeps the plans · Time & Weather window, §47 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** On turn 2 or later, open Time & Weather and press Randomize weather a few times. The weather type, clouds, wind, temperature, altimeter and turbulence change each press, mostly staying near the turn's weather. REJECT leaves the turn's weather as it was. ACCEPT puts the shown weather on the turn, the ATO keeps every package, and the generated mission flies that weather. The window has no RE-ROLL TURN button, and the main bar's button reads Re-plan RED.

**Fail signature:** A press leaves the form unchanged or leaves stale fields (an old column still showing), REJECT changes the weather, ACCEPT drops or re-plans packages, or the mission's weather differs from what the window showed.
