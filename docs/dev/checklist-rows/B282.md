### B282 — The new stock AI jets spawn armed · Factions (F/A-18A, Su-25TM, Mirage F1CR/F1JA/F1AZ) · ☐ UNTESTED

Start a Bluefor Cold War or USN 1985 game, frag one flight of each new jet, generate, and open the `.miz` in the Mission Editor (or spawn in and look at the jets on the ramp).

**Pass:** each flight carries its `Retribution <task>` loadout; the F1JA has an R550 Magic on each wingtip (pydcs lists no wingtip pylon for it, so Retribution writes them anyway); the F/A-18A has no FLIR pod on the centerline.

**Fail signature:** a jet spawns clean or with DCS's default fit; the F1JA wingtips are empty; `dcs.log` rejects a pylon on one of these types.
