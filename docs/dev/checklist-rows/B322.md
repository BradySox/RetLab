### B322 — Saved loadouts list, step up, and fly as a squadron's fit · §128 · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-11-08):** the payload tab saves a member's stations as a named fit in `Saved Games\DCS\Retribution\saved_loadouts.json`, lists it as `Saved: <name>` for that airframe in every campaign, and can make it one blue squadron's fit for one task. RetLab no longer reads `MissionEditor\UnitPayloads`. See `docs/dev/retlab-features.md` §128.

**Pass:** on a blue F-16 CAS flight in a 2020 campaign, tick Use custom loadout, put AIM-9M on station 1, click Save loadout as... and name it. Open a new F-16 flight in another campaign: `Saved: <name>` is in the Loadout list and picking it loads AIM-9X on station 1. In a 1985 campaign the generated mission carries an older Sidewinder there. Pick it on a CAS flight, click Use for squadron's CAS, end the turn: the next turn's CAS flights of that squadron show `Saved: <name>`; red F-16s and the squadron's BARCAP flights keep the shipped fit. Delete saved: the squadron note says the fit was deleted and new flights take the shipped fit.

**Fail signature:** a saved fit appears under a `Retribution ...` name, a red flight carries it, an AIM-9M stays a 9M in a 2020 mission, the Loadout list shows a Mission Editor fit from `UnitPayloads`, or the squadron default survives a delete.
