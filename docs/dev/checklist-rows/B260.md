### B260 — Vacuum calls red radars by bullseye · §118 Vacuum ELINT · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-20):** an ELINT flight type (Vacuum) for the C-130J-30 and KC-135
and a RetLab Features switch, Vacuum ELINT (SkyEye). The planner adds it to the AWACS
package on turns with two or more SEAD, SEAD Escort or DEAD flights (one or more since
2026-10-22).

**Pass:** tick the switch, plan a turn with a SEAD flight and a player strike package, run
SkyEye on the mission link. The ATO shows Vacuum in the AWACS package; the kneeboard comms
list VACUUM (ELINT). On the F10 map it orbits about 20 NM behind the AWACS. Tune Vacuum's
frequency: each red SAM or EWR that comes on is called once, "Vacuum, new emitter, SA-6,
bullseye ..., ...", and the bullseye matches the F10 map. A site that blinks is not called
again. A SAM whose ring covers your route is called on STRIKE too; others are not. Land or
kill Vacuum: the calls stop.

**Fail signature:** no Vacuum in the ATO (no C-130J-30 or KC-135 free); calls with no
bullseye (SkyEye has no bullseye from Tacview); the same site called every few seconds
(on-cycle reset broken); every call on STRIKE; a KC-135 Vacuum acting as a tanker.
