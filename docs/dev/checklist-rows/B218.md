### B218 — The debrief window fits the screen and nothing in it is cut off · §4 / §108 · ☐ UNTESTED

Built 2026-10-11. The Debriefing window used to open as one tall column with the
report cards and both lost-units lists squeezed to two rows. It is now two columns.
Unit-tested (`tests/qt_ui/test_debriefing_window.py`) and rendered off-screen with
made-up data; never seen after a flown mission.

- **Setup:** fly any turn where both sides lose units and a few packages fly. Accept
  the results.
- **Pass:** the window opens wider than it is tall, with no picture at the top. Left:
  Mission Impact, Campaign consequences, the cruise missile and CSAR boxes, then the
  two lost-units lists side by side, each filling its box. Right: Flight report cards,
  top to bottom. A lost-units list with 10 rows or fewer has no scrollbar. Each card
  leads with the task and the package target, the grade at the right, and the
  squadron and aircraft on the line under it.
- **Fail signatures:** a lost-units list showing two rows beside an empty frame; a
  card titled with a raw group name such as `Target Anti-ship|12|FA-18C_hornet|`
  (expected only on a debrief reopened from a save made before this change); the
  Okay button off the bottom of the screen.
