### B200 — A second Refuel stays only when the jet needs it, and the tab says why · tanker box · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** in any campaign with a theater tanker, tick **Refuel before the push** on a
strike, SEAD or escort flight, and **Refuel before station** on a TARCAP. Each flight
that gets home on the first top-off has no Refuel after the Split (or after Race-track
end). A flight that keeps the second Refuel shows a bold line under the tick box naming
how many lb under its reserve it would land without it. Unticking removes the line.

**Fail signature:** a TARCAP keeps its Refuel off station while its Payload tab fuel
readout shows a positive margin without it; a flight keeps the second Refuel with no
line under the tick box; the line stays after the box is unticked.
