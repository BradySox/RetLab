### B264 — Debrief stops the SkyEye Take off started, after a restart too · §120 voice bots · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-21):** the SkyEye Take off starts is remembered in
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\DCSRetribution\skyeye-takeoff.json`; debrief closes its console first and
forces it only after 5 s.

**Pass:** with Voice bots on and SkyEye closed, press Take off and wait for the SkyEye
console. Close Retribution, open it again, load the save, and press Take off: no second
SkyEye console opens. Fly briefly and finish: when the results come in, SkyEye's console
closes, and the SRS server's client list drops "GCI ... [BOT]" at once rather than after a
timeout. RetLab's log has no "SkyEye quit ... after starting" line for that stop. Then start
SkyEye yourself (`Start SkyEye.bat`), Take off and debrief: your SkyEye keeps running.

**Fail signature:** a SkyEye still open after the debrief; two SkyEye bots on the SRS
server; the log shows "closing it by force" every time (the console ignored the close
request, so SkyEye never signs off); your own SkyEye closed at debrief.
