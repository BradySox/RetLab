`%LOCALAPPDATA%\DCSRetribution\skyeye-takeoff.json`; debrief closes its console first and
forces it only after 5 s.

in the SkyEye log (its console is hidden since 2026-10-24). Close Retribution, open it again, load the save, and press Take off: no second
`skyeye.exe` starts (Task Manager). Fly briefly and finish: when the results come in, `skyeye.exe`
exits, and the SRS server's client list drops "GCI ... [BOT]" at once rather than after a
timeout. RetLab's log has no "SkyEye quit ... after starting" line for that stop. Then start
SkyEye yourself (`Start SkyEye.bat`), Take off and debrief: your SkyEye keeps running.

server; the log shows "closing it by force" every time (the console ignored the close
request, so SkyEye never signs off); your own SkyEye closed at debrief.
