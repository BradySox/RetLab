### B251 — One raid draws at most 4 alert jets · §1 · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-18, test 62):** the QRA dispatcher sent one defender per attacker in a
detected raid, escorts included, and reached back to every squadron inside the 200 NM GCI
radius once the nearest was spent. Red launched 32 alert jets from 10 squadrons in 15 minutes.
A raid now draws at most 4 defenders alive and engaged at once; a defender lost or sent home
is replaced up to that cap. The harness pins the arithmetic; Moose's real raid clustering is
not tested.

**Pass:** a mission where blue sends two or more large packages (8+ jets) into red airspace.

- Each raid draws 1 to 4 red alert jets. Tacview: count `Intercept|` groups launched in the
  minutes after a package crosses into red's defended airspace.
- Red alert squadrons not near any raid keep their jets on the ground.
- A defender shot down is replaced by one more launch, not by a squadron's worth.

**Fail signature:** 5 or more alert jets launched against one raid at the same time; alert
squadrons far from any raid emptied; no alert launch at all against a raid inside 200 NM.
