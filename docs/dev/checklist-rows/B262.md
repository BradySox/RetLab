### B262 — Take off starts SRS, SkyEye and the SRS client · §120 voice bots · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-20):** File > Voice bots, a machine preference, off by default.

**Pass:** close SRS and SkyEye. Tick the box, point the folder at SkyEye, Save. Press Take
off: the SRS server opens, a minimized SkyEye console appears in the taskbar, the SRS client
opens and connects to 127.0.0.1:5002 by itself. Start the mission and check in with SkyEye.
When the results come in, SkyEye's console closes and SRS stays up. Repeat with everything
already running: nothing new starts, nothing restarts.

**Fail signature:** the client opens but never connects (its Auto Connect option is off, or
the message reached it before its listener opened); a second SkyEye starts; Take off hangs
while SRS starts; SkyEye stays open after the debrief.
