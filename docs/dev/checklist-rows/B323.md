### B323 — Civil traffic flows all mission, clear of the war · §129 · ☐ UNTESTED

Fly any modern-map mission at least 90 minutes with Civilian air traffic on Light (Mission
Generation > General). Watch the F10 map and the `Civil traffic:` lines in `dcs.log`.

**Pass:**
- About 5 civil aircraft are in the air at mission start, and about 5 are still in the air
  at 60 and 90 minutes.
- Airliners are Yak-40s, plus An-26s only on Caucasus, Kola, Afghanistan and Syria. No
  IL-76, An-30 or C-130.
- New airliners appear at the edge of the map or take off from a field neither side uses.
  None appear in the middle of the map after the start.
- No civil track comes within 40 NM of the front or crosses a SAM ring shown on your
  map.
- A civil flight that lands is gone about 2 minutes later, and one that reaches the map
  edge is removed there (`removed, landed` / `removed, left the map` in the log).

**Fail signature:** the sky empties as the mission goes on; an airliner pops in mid-map; a
civil track over the front or inside a SAM ring; two civil aircraft taking off together
from one runway; a Lua error naming `Civil traffic`.
