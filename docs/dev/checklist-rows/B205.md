### B205 — A CAP over its own field is at altitude by its on-station time · §6 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Try:** any campaign where a BARCAP patrols over the field it takes off from (Anatolian Reach: red's Diyarbakir or Aleppo CAP). Open the flight's Waypoints tab before flying, then watch that flight in the mission or in Tacview. ~15 min.

**Pass:** the Waypoints tab shows RACETRACK START about 10 minutes after TAKEOFF for a 25,000-30,000 ft station, not seconds after. In the mission the flight is level at its patrol altitude within a minute or two of that time. A CAP with a long first leg keeps the times it had before.

**Fail signature:** RACETRACK START seconds after TAKEOFF; the flight still climbing several minutes past its on-station time (the 3,000 ft/min estimate is too fast); or on station more than about 5 minutes early (too slow). Note the aircraft type and the two times.
