### B229 — Territory fill on the F10 map and the app map · §111 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** any campaign with a front, generate and open the mission. Abchazian Blitz turn 1
matches the Reddit map that started this.

- The F10 map fills blue ground blue-grey and red ground orange-brown, under every other drawing.
- The edge between the two runs through the red front line.
- A country holding no base (Turkey and Armenia on Abchazian Blitz) is not filled.
- The F10 map pans and zooms as smoothly as before. Red Tide has the largest piece, about 1,650 corners.
- In the app, the Territory layer shows the same fill; ticking Sectors draws the sector edges, and names appear from zoom 9.
- After a base changes hands, the next turn's fill follows it.

**Fail signature:** no fill at all (`Territory could not be built` in the log); a fill on
top of the unit icons; a filled sea or a filled neutral country; stutter or a long load on
opening the F10 map; two sides overlapping as an olive-green band.
