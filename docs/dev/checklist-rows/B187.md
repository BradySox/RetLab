### B187 — Carrier flights spawn early enough to launch on time · §104 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** in a campaign with a carrier package of several flights (Iron Gate's
ANTEATER-style SEAD 4-ship plus escort pairs), the Waypoints tab shows each carrier
flight's takeoff 2 min + 75 s per jet after its startup, and later flights off the same
boat spawn earlier by the launches ahead of them. In Tacview the last jet of each flight
is airborne within about 2 minutes of its planned takeoff, and the package joins on
time.

**Fail signature:** carrier flights still launching 9-15 min after their planned
takeoff (test 54); flights from the same boat spawning at the same moment and jamming the
deck; an airfield flight's wait changing because of carrier traffic.
