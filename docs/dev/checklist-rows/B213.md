### B213 — A Tomcat can dial the carrier's Link 4 and gets ACLS and the data link picture · §65 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** any campaign with a player F-14 on a Nimitz-class carrier or the Forrestal.

- The CV Operations Data kneeboard page shows Link 4 as 320.x (Roosevelt .1, Lincoln .2,
  Washington .3, Stennis .4, Truman .5, Forrestal .9).
- The RIO sets that number on the Data Link Control Panel, mode ON (Link 4A), and the
  carrier's tracks appear on the TID.
- On a Case III recovery with ACL selected, the ACLS needles come alive.
- A Hornet on the same boat still gets ACLS, and its DTC carries the 320.x number.
- A campaign saved before 2026-10-10 shows 320.x after its next mission is generated.

**Fail signature:** the kneeboard still reading 336.x; a Tomcat with the right number set
and no tracks or no ACLS; a Hornet that lost ACLS.
