### B197 — ASAP tankers, AWACS and the first CAP launch with an early mission start · §104 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Try:** Long Road to H3 with a strike flight ticked **Refuel before the push** (it starts the mission early). Generate and fly or watch in Tacview.

**Pass:** the mission starts before the turn's clock, and the KC-135s, the E-3A and the first BARCAPs are airborne from that start, not from the turn's clock. The refuelling strike reaches the tanker box after the tanker does, plugs in, and makes its Join on time. With no flight that needs an early start, everything launches at the turn's clock as before.

**Fail signature:** the tankers or AWACS spawn at the turn's clock while the mission began earlier; the strike reaches an empty box; or the mission still starts early after the flight that needed it is gone.
