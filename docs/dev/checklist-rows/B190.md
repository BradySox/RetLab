### B190 — The Threat Intel Brief names each SAM correctly and its advice matches the system · Threat Intel Brief (§4) · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** on any campaign with known enemy SAMs, the kneeboard's Threat Intel Brief shows each system by NATO name (`SA-10 "Grumble"`, not a radar name), Radar/Heat guidance that fits it, the ceiling and RWR symbol from the Soviet SAMs guide, and a "How to beat it" line for that system. The RWR symbol matches what the jet's RWR shows for that site. Unidentified sites still list their bullseye positions.

**Fail signature:** a radar SAM described as a heat-seeker or a gun, MANPADS advice on a long-range SAM, a "HARM" number on a card, or a card named after a search radar while its SAM sits on the same site.
