### B234 — An empty player flight's AI twin launches; a manned one's stays parked · §113 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** in a multiplayer mission (or a single-player one with two player flights), tick
*AI fills if no player* on two player flights. Fly one; leave the other's seats empty.

- 5 minutes in, the coalition sees "<callsign> has no players. AI flies its tasking (...)".
- At the empty flight's planned takeoff, an `AI ...` group appears in the air over its home
  base and flies the flight's route and tasking to the target.
- The manned flight's twin never appears, and no message names it.
- The kneeboard Mission Info page of both flights has the OPTIONAL line.
- A part-player flight (player lead, AI wingmen) left empty: note whether the AI wingmen
  launch, and whether the one-seat twin flies alongside them.
- After the debrief, a shot-down twin costs its squadron the airframe; the empty client
  group costs nothing.

**Fail signature:** the twin never launches, or launches with a player in the flight; the
twin appears on the ground or at mission start; a message for a manned flight; the empty
client group counted as lost.
