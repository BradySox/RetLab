### B206 — A theater tanker flies a racetrack you can fill up on, and the map drag moves and swings it · tanker track · ☐ UNTESTED

Built 2026-10-09 (DM call) after the four-corner tanker box failed in flight (row B153):
every theater tanker flies a 40 NM racetrack again, on DCS's own Orbit task. Unit-tested
(`test_theater_tanker_track.py`); not flown since the return, and no recording has a jet
on the boom of a racetrack tanker. Design: `docs/dev/design/retlab-tanker-box-notes.md`.

**Pass (flight):** join a theater KC-135 as it rolls out of a turn. It flies wings level
for about 6 minutes (40 NM), turns once through 180 degrees, and repeats. You fill up on
one leg. Note its bank in a turn with you on the boom.

**Pass (map):** a new turn's theater tanker shows RACETRACK START and RACETRACK END.
Dragging the first point moves both; dragging the second swings the track around the
first and keeps it 40 NM long; each marker's tooltip says which. The F10 marker and the
cockpit box follow the dragged track.

**Pass (old save):** a save from 2026-09-28 to 2026-10-09 with a theater tanker loads,
and that turn's tanker flies a racetrack on the box's old front leg.

**Fail signature:** the tanker weaves or reverses its turn with a jet on the boom; a
level leg far short of 6 minutes; a theater tanker showing BOX points on a new turn; a
dragged end point leaving the track longer or shorter; an old save failing to load.
