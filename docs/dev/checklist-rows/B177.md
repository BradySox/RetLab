### B177 — Hornet radio presets carry names from the cartridge · §74 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** in a player F/A-18C with the DTC loaded, the COMM1/COMM2 presets the kneeboard
comm plan lists show names: your flight's callsign (5 letters, e.g. `ENFIE`), the AWACS
and tanker callsigns, `PKG`, `JTAC`, `DEP`/`ARR`/`DVT`, on the same channel numbers and
frequencies as the kneeboard. Channels the plan does not use read `CH n`.

**Fail signature:** every preset reads `CH n`; a named channel whose frequency differs
from the kneeboard; or the cartridge refused on load.
