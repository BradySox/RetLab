### B258 — The ABCCC C-130 runs the package on STRIKE · §117 package control · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-20):** an ABCCC flight type for the C-130J-30 and a RetLab
Features switch, ABCCC C-130 (SkyEye). The planner adds one to the AWACS package; it orbits 30 NM
outside the threat rings with the package's 2-ship escort on it. While it is airborne it runs every player package on STRIKE (common) through
RetLab's SkyEye copy; the AWACS works STRIKE trimmed, in the other voice.

**Pass:** tick the switch, generate a mission with a player strike package that has SEAD and an
AI flight, run SkyEye with `mission-link-address: 127.0.0.1:42690`. The kneeboard comms list
STRIKE (Anvil). Tune STRIKE: "Anvil, <you>, check in" gets radar contact, the hold
angels and the push time; "say push time" and "ready to push" answer. Three minutes before the push: "All players, check in"; answer "ready" and get "copy
ready", with the AI flights read out. Stay silent once: the push holds or goes without you
by your role. At the push time: one
"push". Leave the SEAD on the ground: two holds of 3 minutes, the AI flight orbits its HOLD each
time, then an abort sends the AI flight home. On a clean run "ready to push" releases the AI from
its HOLD. The AWACS speaks on STRIKE only for threat and merge calls and your own picture or
bogey calls, in the other voice. Kill or land the C-130: package calls stop. On the F10 map the ABCCC orbits about 30 NM outside the red
threat rings, ahead of the AWACS, and its 2-ship escort stays on it for the whole station.

**Fail signature:** no voice (SkyEye not on the mission link); text instead of voice (LuaSocket
blocked); a spoken call also shown as on-screen text (since 2026-10-24 voice only); the
C-130 shows the EW menu or talks as a DCS AWACS; the AI ignores a hold or abort; the AI never
leaves HOLD after "ready to push" (the `SwitchWaypoint` index is off by one); the AWACS repeats
pictures or faded calls on STRIKE. The escort leaves the ABCCC for the AWACS or goes home early.
