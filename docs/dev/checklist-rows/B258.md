### B258 — The ABCCC C-130 runs the package on STRIKE · §117 package control · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-20):** a Package Control flight type for the C-130J-30 and a RetLab
Features switch, Package Control C-130 (SkyEye). The planner plans one per side, one slot back
from the AWACS. While it is airborne it runs every player package on STRIKE (common) through
RetLab's SkyEye copy; the AWACS works STRIKE trimmed, in the other voice.

**Pass:** tick the switch, generate a mission with a player strike package that has SEAD and an
AI flight, run SkyEye with `mission-link-address: 127.0.0.1:42690`. The kneeboard comms list
STRIKE (Bookshelf). Tune STRIKE: "Bookshelf, <you>, check in" gets radar contact, the hold
angels and the push time; "say push time" and "ready to push" answer. At the push time: "push,
push". Leave the SEAD on the ground: two holds of 3 minutes, the AI flight orbits its HOLD each
time, then an abort sends the AI flight home. On a clean run "ready to push" releases the AI from
its HOLD. The AWACS speaks on STRIKE only for threat and merge calls and your own picture or
bogey calls, in the other voice. Kill or land the C-130: package calls stop.

**Fail signature:** no voice but text (SkyEye not on the mission link, or LuaSocket blocked); the
C-130 shows the EW menu or talks as a DCS AWACS; the AI ignores a hold or abort; the AI never
leaves HOLD after "ready to push" (the `SwitchWaypoint` index is off by one); the AWACS repeats
pictures or faded calls on STRIKE.
