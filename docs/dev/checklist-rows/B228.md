### B228 — An alert squadron launches no more jets than its reserve · §1 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** any campaign where an enemy alert (QRA) squadron scrambles single jets and
loses them in the air. Operation Desert Aladeen turn 1 (test 56): Kirkuk's JF-17 squadron,
reserve 4. Count the squadron's scramble clones in Tacview or `state.json`
(`Intercept|<base>|<squadron id>#NNN-01`).

- The squadron launches at most its reserve over the whole mission.
- After its last jet is shot down, no further clone of that squadron appears.
- A jet that lands and shuts down can launch again later.
- `intercept_survivors` for the squadron matches reserve minus jets lost.

**Fail signature:** more clone numbers than the reserve (test 56: `#001` to `#008` from a
reserve of 4); a new clone 20 to 30 seconds after a kill of the same squadron.
