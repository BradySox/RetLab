### B247 — A downed pilot never captures a base · base capture, CSAR · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-18):** on test 59 an ejected F-15E crew landed inside an empty
Gazipasa and captured it for blue. The capture trigger now ends with a Lua check
(`capture_zone_taken` in `dcs_retribution.lua`) that leaves downed pilots of both sides
out. It runs as a DCS LUA PREDICATE condition, which the harness cannot exercise.

**Pass:** any campaign with CSAR on.

- A pilot who comes down within 1.9 mi of an enemy base with no defenders left does not
  capture it: no capture event in `state.json`, and the base keeps its owner next turn.
- Ground forces that reach an empty enemy base still capture it (a normal front-line
  capture, or CTLD troops).
- `dcs.log` shows no Lua error naming `capture_zone_taken` or `c_predicate`.

**Fail signature:** a capture event for a base where only a `CSAR ...` or
`Pilot ...kHz` group stood; a base that ground forces cleared and occupied but never
flipped (the predicate failing, so no capture can happen at all).
