### B315 — F-15EX V3 jets spawn, load and fire · F-15EX V3 mod · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-11-06):** RetLab supports the F-15EX V3 mod's F-15EX (CFTs) and F-15IA, with its own A2A loadouts (`retlab-f15ex-v3-mod-notes.md`).

**Pass:** with the mod installed and ticked in New Game, a USA 2020 or Israel 2017 campaign fields the jets. In DCS each flight spawns with 10 AMRAAMs (AIM-260A on F-15EX Escort and Fighter sweep), 2 AIM-9X or Python-5 and 3 tanks, every store visible on the jet, and AI flights fire them at red fighters. A player slot shows the same loadout in the rearm screen.

**Fail signature:** a station is empty or the DCS log says a store cannot be equipped; the jet spawns clean; the mission fails to load the unit type.
