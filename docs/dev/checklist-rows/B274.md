### B274 — Shrike seeker head matches the target SAM · §8 · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-22):** a Shrike's RF Guidance Unit is set from the radars at the
package target, with or without the date rule: Mk 49 Mod 1 for SA-3/SA-6, Mk 25 for SA-2,
Mk 50 for a site no rule names. A head set by hand in Weapon Settings is kept.

**Pass:** frag an F-4E SEAD or DEAD on an SA-2 site with Shrikes on the pylons, with
Restrict weapons by date off.
- The pylon's Weapon Settings window shows Mk 25 before generation.
- In the generated miz (or the ME's weapon settings for that jet) the Shrike carries Mk 25,
  and the Attack Profile is whatever the window showed.
- In flight the Shrike tones on and guides to the Fan Song.
- Set a second jet's head to Mk 23 by hand: its miz carries Mk 23.

**Fail signature:** the miz carries Mk 22 against an SA-2; the hand-set Mk 23 comes out
Mk 25; Attack Profile reverts to Direct after choosing Loft; DCS rejects the pylon's settings
table (store missing at spawn).
