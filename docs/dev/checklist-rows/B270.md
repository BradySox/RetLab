### B270 — A SAM no radar can see over wakes up on its own · Skynet IADS · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-21):** a ground early-warning radar covers a SAM only if it has line
of sight to a point 10,000 ft above the SAM. A SAM with no such radar (and no AWACS in range)
fights on its own radar as plain DCS AI. Test 63: MUDSKIPPER's SA-11 sat dark all mission
under a Box Spring 82 NM away behind the Taurus.

**Pass:** tick the Skynet debug option for red. Fly at a red SAM whose nearest early-warning
radar is far away or behind a ridge (test 63's Turkish-coast SA-11 is the reference). Its
radar shows on the RWR from mission start, and it fires once you are inside its envelope.
A SAM with a radar close by on open ground still stays dark until you are in range.

**Fail signature:** the far SAM never shows on the RWR and never fires (the old behavior);
every red SAM radiates from mission start (the check blocks too much, e.g. a bad mast or
height value); a Lua error in dcs.log naming `canSeeAboveSite` or `land.isVisible`.
