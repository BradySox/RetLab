### B308 — Re-plan keeps the player's packages · top bar Re-plan... button · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** On turn 2 or later, take a seat in one auto-planned strike package and edit its loadout. Press Re-plan..., then Re-plan BLUE. Your package is still there with its loadout and TOT, and no second package goes after the same target with the same job. The all-AI blue packages come back re-planned. Re-plan RED leaves every blue package alone; Re-plan both does both. Cancel changes nothing. The generated mission flies.

**Fail signature:** The kept package is gone or changed, a second package hits its target, a squadron shows more aircraft or pilots tasked than it owns, or Re-plan BLUE re-plans red.
