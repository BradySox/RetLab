### B179 — Each orbit in a package ends at its own time · upstream #964 port · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** in a package holding an AWACS or tanker and a BARCAP, each stays on its
orbit until its own patrol end time on the flight's Waypoints tab. In the miz, each
orbiting group has its own `StopOrbit<group id>-<seconds>` trigger.

**Fail signature:** the AWACS or tanker leaves station when the BARCAP's patrol ends;
an orbit that never ends (the trigger fired but the orbit did not stop); or two
groups sharing one `StopOrbit` trigger.
