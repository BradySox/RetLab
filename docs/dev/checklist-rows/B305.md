### B305 — Spangdahlem Approach and Departure use the 52 TFW SOP fixes · §119 step 2 · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-11-02):** Spangdahlem (Fulda Gap 1985) has a chart from the squadron's SOP: ALPHA, LAKE and BANGOR enter for runway 23, BRAVO and TUCSON for runway 05, all at 2,600 ft, and every fix is a departure exit. See `docs/dev/retlab-features.md` §119.

**Pass:** in Fulda Gap 1985 with Voice ATC on, take off from Spangdahlem and call Departure: the exit you get is one of the five fixes. Come back from the east on runway 23 and call Approach: you get ALPHA, LAKE or BANGOR, maintain 2,600. The ATC kneeboard card lists the five fixes. With TACAN 32X tuned, ALPHA reads about 088/9 and LAKE about 029/8.

**Fail signature:** Approach gives a generic "10 miles east of the field" point; a runway 23 arrival gets BRAVO or TUCSON; a fix's TACAN radial or distance is off by more than 2 degrees or 1 mile.
