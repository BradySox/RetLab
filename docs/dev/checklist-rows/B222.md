### B222 — A side with an AWACS and no ground radar scrambles its alert fighters · §1 · ☐ UNTESTED

Built 2026-10-11 on the DM's call after test 55: blue had two E-2Cs, no EWR, and logged
`Intercept: no detection sources for BLUE; QRA will not scramble.` The QRA plugin now adds
the side's own AWACS groups to its detection set. Harness-tested
(`tests/lua/test_intercept_filter.py`); never flown.

**Pass:** a campaign where one side has an AWACS up, an alert reserve, and no EWR or
search-radar SAM (Vectron's Claw blue). Let the enemy fly a strike, BAI or other
air-to-ground package toward that side's bases.

- `dcs.log` has `Intercept: <SIDE> defends N zone(s)` for that side and no
  `no detection sources for <SIDE>` line.
- Alert fighters scramble from the nearest alert base once the raid is inside the AWACS
  picture and the scramble radius.
- After the AWACS is shot down or goes home, no new scramble launches.
- A side with ground radars scrambles as before.

**Fail signature:** `no detection sources` with an AWACS on station; alert jets launching
with no AWACS and no radar alive; a scramble against a CAP or sweep (the task filter is
unchanged and should still ignore them).
