### B318 — Stock-livery squadron presets wear their paint · squadron presets · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** start a new game with a USA faction that flies the F-15E and one that flies the
F-100D or P-51D. In the Air Wing dialog, pick the 492th Fighter Squadron (F-15E) and one of the
new F-100D or P-51D squadrons. Generate a turn and look at the jets in the mission editor or
on the ramp: each 492nd jet wears a Madhatters skin, several different ones across a
four-ship, and the new squadron wears its unit's paint.

**Fail signature:** a new squadron's jets in default paint (the livery name does not match the
folder in your install); two jets of one 492nd four-ship in identical skins every mission; an
F-14 squadron whose lead jet does not carry the lowest board number.
