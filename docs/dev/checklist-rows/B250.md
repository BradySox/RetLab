### B250 — Heatblur squadrons wear with the hours they fly · §43 · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-18):** each F-4E and A-6E squadron keeps a wear number in the save.
After each mission, airborne hours from the sortie records add 10 per hour per airframe the
squadron owns; every turn takes 5 off; it stays between 0 and 50. Its untouched player jets
spawn with that Wear and Tear. Unit tests pin the arithmetic; real sortie records and what
DCS does with the value are not tested.

**Pass:** a campaign with a blue F-4E squadron, over 3 or more turns.

- On turn 1 the payload tab shows Aircraft Wear and Tear 0.
- After a turn where the squadron flew several long sorties, a new F-4E flight shows a higher
  Wear and Tear, and the generated `.miz` carries the same value on the player jet.
- After turns with no F-4E sorties, the value falls back toward 0.
- A Wear and Tear typed on the payload tab is what the `.miz` gets.

**Fail signature:** Wear stays 0 after the squadron flew; it climbs past 50; a squadron that
stood down for several turns does not recover; an older save fails to load.
