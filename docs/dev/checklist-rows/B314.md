### B314 — FIXED sites fit a different faction's SAM · §122 · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-11-06):** a drawn FIXED site leaves out support units its owner cannot field (search radar, command post, power, point-defense gun). When the owner cannot field the drawn launchers or tracking radar, its own system for the task goes into the drawn spots: a launcher on every launcher spot, each radar and command post on the closest radar spot, any left over about 130 ft beside the site. Before, both cases built a stock site.

**Pass:** start Fulda Gap 1985 or Anatolian Reach with a red faction other than the recommended one. The New Game log shows `FIXED site ... takes the drawn spots` lines. In DCS those sites have the faction's launchers on the drawn launcher spots, their radar comes up, and they engage.

**Fail signature:** a site is missing or is a stock circle away from the drawn spots; its radar never comes on (a required radar or command post left out); units overlap so badly that one blows up at mission start.
