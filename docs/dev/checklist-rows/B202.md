### B202 — Minutes on the tanker moves the push, not the TOT · tanker box · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** tick **Refuel before the push** on a strike or escort flight. **Minutes on the
tanker** shows 9 for a 2-ship (17 for a 4-ship). Type 30: Hold departs 21 minutes
earlier, the Join and the TOT do not move, and **Reset** puts 9 back. The same on a
CAP's **Refuel before station** moves takeoff, not the race-track times. Fly it or
watch in Tacview: the flight reaches the Join on time after tanking.

**Fail signature:** the TOT or the Join time moves; the box can be changed with the tick
box clear; the typed number is gone after reopening the flight or reloading the save.
