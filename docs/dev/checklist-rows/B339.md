### B339 — F-4E fuel plan matches a flown sortie · §46 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Setup:** any F-4E player flight, cold start, the route as planned. Note the Payload tab line ("lands with about X lb") before you fly; record Tacview.

**Pass:** the fuel on landing is within about 2,000 lb of the plan's figure on a sortie flown along the route (no long detours, no tanker). `python tools/tacview_fuel_rates.py --pilot <your pilot name> --type F-4E <file>` reads the rates if it is off.

**Fail signature:** you land with far less than the line said (the old walk showed about half the real burn), or the line says "Tanker: not needed" on a sortie that could not get home without it.
