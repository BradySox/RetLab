### B344 — A moving short-range SAM stops to fight, then drives on · §131 · ☐ UNTESTED

A friendly or enemy Osa, Tor or Shilka battery ordered to move, and a flight that flies at it
while it is on the road. Record Tacview and keep `dcs.log`.

**Pass:**
- `dcs.log` has `SAM_TRANSIT_DEFENSE|<group>|DEFENDING` when the jet enters its envelope.
- The column halts, the radar comes on and it fires (Tacview).
- After a quiet spell the log shows it packing, the radar goes off, and it drives on along
  the same route from where it stopped, not from the start.
- A battery outside the reviewed list (an SA-11 with a towed piece, an RBS-98) stays packed
  and holds fire.

**Fail signature:** the battery never fires at a jet over it; it fires from a long range it
cannot reach; it stays deployed for the rest of the mission; it restarts its route from the
first waypoint; a `SAM_TRANSIT_DEFENSE` Lua error.
