### B210 — The A-10's gun carries the ammunition picked on the Payload tab · §73 · ☐ UNTESTED

Built 2026-10-10 from juanjux/dcs-escalation #515, not flown there or here. The Payload
tab of an A-10A, A-10C or A-10C II has a GAU-8 ammunition box (Combat Mix, HEI, Target
Practice); the choice rides on the loadout and is written to the unit's
`payload.ammo_type`. Unit-tested (`tests/test_gau8_ammunition.py`,
`tests/qt_ui/test_gau8_payload_editor.py`), and the three indices were read off the
local DCS install.

- **Setup:** a player A-10C II flight. Payload tab → tick Use custom loadout → GAU-8
  ammunition → HEI High Explosive Incendiary. Generate and fly to a ground target.
- **Pass (app):** the box is greyed until Use custom loadout is ticked. Another
  airframe's Payload tab has no such box.
- **Pass (flight):** the rearm window on the ramp shows HEI as the gun's ammunition.
- **Fail signatures:** the gun loads Combat Mix whatever was picked; the rearm window
  shows a blank or wrong gun entry; an A-10 flight left on the default loadout spawns
  with an empty gun.
