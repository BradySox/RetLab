### B217 — The mission records who killed what, and the AI link's last turn sorts losses by cause · §109 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** any mission with an air-to-air kill, a SAM kill and a ground-attack kill. After
the turn, open `http://localhost:16880/retribution-ai/prev_turns?token=...` (Developer
tools > Copy AI connect link gives the address and token).

- `state.json` from the mission has a `kill_causes` list, each entry with `target`,
  `killer`, and usually `killer_type` and `weapon`.
- `last_turn.red_lost_to` and `blue_lost_to` are there, and the cause of a loss you
  watched matches: a jet shot down by a fighter under `aircraft`, one killed by a SAM
  under `sam`.
- `red_air_killers` names the type that did the shooting; `red_air_weapons` the missile.
- The cause totals are not higher than `red_lost` / `blue_lost`.
- The debrief window and the kneeboard SITREP read as before.

**Fail signature:** no `kill_causes` in `state.json`; every cause `unknown`; a kill by your
own flight filed under `own_side`; a script error naming `record_kill_cause` in `dcs.log`.
