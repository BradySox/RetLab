### B342 — A reserve leaves base stock, drives out, and joins a front it reaches · §131 · ☐ UNTESTED

A new game with a friendly base holding spare armor (tanks plus a towed gun battery) near an
active front. Record Tacview.

**Pass:**
- The base window shows **Deploy reserve...**. Send a few tanks and one gun to a point
  between the base and the front; Preview lists the truck added, the miles and the turns.
- The base's stock drops by those units; a new group appears with miles to go.
- In the next mission the column spawns on its road where it is and drives on (Tacview).
- On arrival the group sits on clear ground at the point, vehicles not on top of each other.
- When your front advances over the group, a message says it joined the front; its units
  are back in that base's stock and the group is gone from the map.
- A campaign armor group the front passes stays where it is.

**Fail signature:** no Deploy reserve button with Ground movement on; stock not debited, or
debited twice; units stacked at the point; a group swept by the front stays on the map or
its units vanish; a campaign armor group gets pulled into a base.
