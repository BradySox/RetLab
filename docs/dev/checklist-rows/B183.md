### B183 — A strike on a motorpool depot hits the parked vehicles · §56 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** in Red Tide with armor parked at the Haina depot, frag an AI Strike and an AI BAI
package at it and fly or watch a turn. The Strike flight drops on individual parked
vehicles, not one spot in the middle; the BAI flight's kneeboard and DTC show one waypoint
named like any BAI target; the parked vehicles stay in their grid while bombs fall. The
debrief lists the dead vehicles as motorpool losses.

**Fail signature:** bombs on the grid's centre with vehicles untouched around it; a BAI
waypoint per unit type; vehicles scattering out of the grid under attack; or no motorpool
loss rows after hits.
