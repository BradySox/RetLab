### B326 — Base scripts, QRA, relocation and COIN run without MIST · MIST removal step 1 · ☐ UNTESTED

Any campaign with QRA alert flights and an escorted package. Fly 20+ minutes, then end the
mission and let Retribution read the result. A COIN campaign covers the movers; a coastal
map with a ship or ground group placed on the wrong surface covers the relocation.

**Pass:**
- `dcs.log` has no `DCSRetribution|` error lines and no `attempt to index global 'mist'`.
- The state file is written (Retribution reads kills and losses after the mission).
- QRA jets scramble against an intruder; the player alert cue still fires.
- An escort stays with its package (the escort leash).
- The sortie record shows tracks sampled through the flight.
- Logged `water_relocate: relocating` / `land_relocate: relocating` groups sit on land / in
  deep water at start.
- COIN: the HVT convoy, a VBIED and a cell drive.

**Fail signature:** `DCSRetribution|write_state_error_handling:` or `DCSRetribution|Intercept:`
errors in `dcs.log`; no state file after the mission; QRA never launches; a relocated group
vanishes or keeps its old position; COIN movers sit still.
