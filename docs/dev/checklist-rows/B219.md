### B219 — Anti-ship flights spread across the ships of a sea group · §8 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** any campaign with an enemy carrier group or surface group of three or more ships,
and at least three anti-ship flights against it (one auto-planned package is two; add a
flight or a second package). Tacview on. No player slot needed; it runs at acceleration.
Design note: `docs/dev/design/retlab-antiship-deal-notes.md`.

- The first two flights fire at the escort with the longest-reach SAM (Pyotr Velikiy, a
  Ticonderoga, a Type 052D), not at the carrier.
- The third and fourth fire at the carrier, and each later flight at a different escort.
- No ship of the group ends the mission never fired at when there were more flights than
  the line needs (2 for the SAM ship, 2 per carrier, 1 per other ship).
- A flight that reaches its launch point after its own ship has sunk fires at the next ship
  in line instead of at the wreck.
- A B-52 or Tu-22M3 flight may split its load between its own ship and the next. Note what
  it does.

**Fail signature:** every flight fires at the same ship again; a flight reaches the ingress
and goes home with its missiles (the per-ship task did not take); missiles launched at a
ship that was already sunk at launch time; `has no attackable target group` in the
Retribution log for a ship-group target.
