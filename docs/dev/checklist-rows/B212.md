### B212 — The AI plans strikes on enemy convoys and cargo ships, and the anti-ship flight attacks · §8 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** any campaign where the enemy has a supply convoy or a cargo ship heading for one of
its front-line bases. Blue plans before red arranges the turn's transports, so blue sees only
the ones made at turn end: ground units red bought last turn setting out on their first leg,
or an ambient supply convoy. A red transport already under way from an earlier turn is not
visible to blue's planner. Red plans after blue and sees every blue transport. Convoys and
cargo ships both enabled in Performance.

- After turn planning, blue's ATO has a BAI package on the enemy convoy or an Anti-ship
  package on the enemy cargo ship, when aircraft are free for it.
- Red's ATO does the same against a blue convoy or cargo ship.
- In the mission, the AI anti-ship flight releases on the cargo ship.
- `dcs.log` and the Retribution log carry no `Unexpected target type for Anti-Ship mission`.

**Fail signature:** no package ever planned on an enemy transport across several turns that
had one; an anti-ship flight that reaches the ingress point and goes home with its weapons;
`has no attackable target group` in the log for a cargo-ship target.
