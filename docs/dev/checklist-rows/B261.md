### B261 — Voice ATC Ground and Tower · §119 Voice ATC · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-20):** Ground and Tower for player flights through SkyEye: taxi,
takeoff, landing, readback checks, scolding calls, an F10 ATC menu.

**Pass:** tick Voice ATC (SkyEye), run SkyEye from `BradySox/skyeye` branch
`retlab/voice-atc` with its mission link, fly solo from one field. The kneeboard has a
"Talking to ATC" card with the field's Ground and Tower frequencies and no squawk, the
comms ladder lists Kobuleti Ground and Tower with preset channels, and those presets are
in the jet's radio. Both are UHF (since 2026-10-21, B266), and they stay the same on the next turn's generate. The Mission Info airfield table and the
departure page's ATC box show GND and TWR with their channels, not the DCS tower. On
Ground, once the jet is powered: "Kobuleti Ground, Viper 1-1, request taxi" gets "taxi to runway 25, hold short, contact Tower", no squawk (since 2026-10-22). A
readback without the runway gets "readback incomplete, hold short runway ..." (Radio discipline
Strict; Relaxed lets it go and only corrects a wrong number; Casual checks nothing). On Tower:
"holding short
runway 25, ready" gets runway, wind, altimeter and "cleared for takeoff". Home at 5 NM:
"5 miles, full stop" gets "cleared to land"; after landing, "clear the runway, taxi to
parking". Taxiing before a taxi clearance gets "not cleared for taxi" once. F10 > ATC makes
the same calls.

After an AI jet takes off at your field, Tower's runway matches the one it used.

**Fail signature:** no ATC reply at all (SkyEye not tuned: check its log for "tuned listen
radios"); replies on the wrong frequency; Whisper never matching the callsign ("say again
your callsign" every time); the wind reading from the wrong direction (declination sign);
DCS's own tower talking on the Ground or Tower frequency; a scolding call repeated, or any
scolding under Relaxed or Casual.
