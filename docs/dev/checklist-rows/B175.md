### B175 — The Viper HSD draws the land borders near the route before the front line · §74 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Setup:** any real-world map with a border within 40 NM of a player F-16C's route (Syria, Iraq, Caucasus, Persian Gulf). DTC on for the flight, National borders ticked (the default). Load the cartridge, open the HSD, and set a range scale that reaches the border.

**Pass:** a gray dashed line lies on the real border (check against the F10 map's country lines), and HSD CNTL → LINE1 toggles it, with a 3-letter country tag on each side (SYR, IRQ) on the right side of the line. The front line, if there is room, is on a later LINE with an FLT tag, and the tanker box has its callsign tag inside. Unticking National borders on the flight's DTC tab puts the front line back on LINE1.

**Fail signature:** a tag on the wrong side of its border; a tag that joins the route; no border line near a border the route passes; a line along a coast or the map edge (a non-shared edge leaked in); or the DTC page refusing the cartridge.
