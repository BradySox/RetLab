### B333 — An Arc Light B-52 flies over its target and the carpet is the whole strike · §32 · ☐ UNTESTED

Any campaign with Arc Light on (1968 Yankee Station) and a B-52 Strike in the ATO. Watch the cell
in F10 or Tacview through the run-in.

**Pass:**
- The B-52s fly the route over the target; none turns home before the target.
- `dcs.log` has `Arc Light carpet released by '<group>'`; the carpet walks across the target.
- The B-52s drop nothing of their own and land with their bombs.

**Fail signature:** the cell turns back before the run-in and no carpet fires; or the B-52s
still release their own bombs on the target.
