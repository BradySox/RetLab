### B340 — A long-road convoy spawns where it stopped and drives on · §131 · ☐ UNTESTED

A new game on a campaign with a supply road longer than 120 miles between two friendly
bases (Syria and Iraq maps have several). Order a ground transfer along it, or wait for one.

**Pass:**
- After the first turn the convoy's card and the Pending transfers list say how far it has
  come and how many miles are left; the units are not at the far base yet.
- In the next mission the column spawns on that road where it stopped, lined up nose to tail,
  and drives on toward the base (Tacview). No vehicle spawns off the road or in a ditch.
- A turn or two later the units arrive at the base. The Cancel button is grey while it is on
  the road.
- A short route (under 120 miles) still arrives in one turn, as before.
- Bombing the column on the road removes those vehicles from the transfer.

**Fail signature:** the column spawns back at its origin every turn; it never arrives; it
spawns spread sideways off the road; units arrive twice or vanish; Cancel teleports them home.
