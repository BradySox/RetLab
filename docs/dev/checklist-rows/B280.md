### B280 — Germany map borders follow the campaign's year · §98 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** on a new Fulda Gap 1985 game, the kneeboard packages map draws a border line along
the inner German border, and with Neutral-faction border defense ticked the F10 map shows
East Germany as its own red-held country. On a new Baltic Fury (2027) game, no line runs
between the old East and West, and the F10 map names one Germany.

**Fail signature:** a 2027 kneeboard or F10 map showing the inner German border or a "GDR"
label; a 1985 map with no line between East and West Germany; Kastrup or Peenemunde outside
any country.
