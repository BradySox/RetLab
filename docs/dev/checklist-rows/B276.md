### B276 — ABCCC says execute at the join point with the package assembled · §117 · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-23):** the ABCCC's one "push" from the HOLD became two calls:
"push to join" leaving the HOLD, and "execute" once every airborne striker and escort is
within 5 NM of the JOIN point. SEAD is not waited for. A late flight gets 3 minutes past the
join time, then is left behind; with no striker at the join the package aborts. AI flights
early at the join orbit there until execute.

**Pass:** fly a player strike package with an AI escort and an AI SEAD, ABCCC airborne.
- At the push time Blackjack says "Ford package, Blackjack, push to join."
- The AI escort reaches the JOIN first and orbits it.
- When you reach the JOIN (inside 5 NM), Blackjack says "Ford package, Blackjack, execute."
  and the escort leaves its orbit for the ingress point.
- The SEAD runs ahead on its own timing; nobody waits for it at the JOIN.
- Second run: hang back 10 NM from the JOIN. About 3 minutes past the join time Blackjack says
  "Ford 1-1, Blackjack, not at join. Package executing without you." then "execute."

**Fail signature:** execute goes out the moment the package pushes (the HOLD sits inside
5 NM of the JOIN); the AI escort never leaves its JOIN orbit after execute (the user-flag stop
condition did not fire); the AI escort flies through the JOIN without orbiting; no execute
call at all.
