### B211 — A saved point's map pin renames, deletes and drags, and My aircraft follows · §102 · ☐ UNTESTED

Built 2026-10-10 from juanjux/dcs-escalation #519. Each saved point is a pin on the map
in its kind's colour, in place of the old dot. Unit-tested on the server, the Qt window
and the client; never seen in the running app. Design:
`docs/dev/design/retlab-my-aircraft-notes.md` §6a.

- **Setup:** a turn with a player flight. Map → crosshair button → click a spot → Save
  as waypoint, twice, and once as an orbit. Open My aircraft beside the map.
- **Pass (pin):** each point is a pin; hovering shows `callsign: name (Kind)`. Clicking
  opens a box with the name, the owner, the coordinates and Rename / Delete.
- **Pass (rename, delete):** Rename changes the name in My aircraft at once. Delete
  removes the pin and the row. Deleting the first point then renaming the second
  renames the second, not a neighbour.
- **Pass (drag):** dragging a pin moves the point; its coordinates and elevation in My
  aircraft change. Dragging an orbit's pin moves the dashed line with it, same heading
  and length. The pin does not jump back a few seconds later.
- **Pass (the other way):** renaming or deleting a point in My aircraft changes the pin
  at once. A new turn or another campaign shows no pins from the old one.
- **Fail signatures:** a pin snaps back after a drag; the wrong point is renamed or
  deleted; the window's selection or open tab resets on a map edit; a cell being typed
  in closes by itself; pins too big to click the base or site under them.
