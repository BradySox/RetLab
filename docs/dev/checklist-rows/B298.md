### B298 — A SAM site with its own generator stays up when the power station dies · Skynet return · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-29):** a site with a live generator unit (Patriot EPP-III, SAMP/T MGE, the S-300 family's 5I57A diesels) is no longer tied to a power station. Bombing the station leaves it running; killing its generator puts it back on the station. See `docs/dev/design/retlab-skynet-return-notes.md`, "Self-powered sites".

**Setup:** an advanced-IADS campaign with a red S-300 (SA-10/SA-20) that fields its two 5I57A diesels, within 35 NM of a red power station. On the map's IADS layer the S-300 draws no power line while its diesels live; a site without diesels nearby still does.

**Pass:** destroy the power station; next turn the S-300 still radiates and engages under Skynet, while the no-generator SAMs behind that station are dark. Then destroy both diesels; the map draws the power line again, and next turn the S-300 is dark because its station is dead.

**Fail signature:** the S-300 goes dark the turn after only the power station dies; or it keeps radiating after both its diesels and the station are dead; or the map still draws a power line to the S-300 while its diesels live.
