### B266 — The ABCCC runs your package, and every voice bot is on a radio you can tune · §117 · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-21):** red's generation pass no longer wipes blue's packages, so the
ABCCC gets the player packages and Vacuum (§118) its STRIKE flags. Voice ATC's (§119) Ground
and Tower moved to UHF. Presets carry only own-side tankers and JTACs and nothing a radio
cannot tune; the F-15E has the support nets on COMM1. AI flights stay off every voice bot
frequency, except that the AI in your package fly on STRIKE with you (2026-10-24). Package control answers the lead's seat ("Ford 5-1"). Voice ATC answers a start
or airborne call.

**Pass:** with ABCCC, Vacuum and Voice ATC on, fly one package with a player F-16 and one
with a player F-15E or F-14. Check in with "<ABCCC>, Ford 5-1, check in" on Strict: the
ABCCC answers with the hold and push time. Ground and Tower answer on their UHF presets
(F-16 COM1, F-15E and F-14 COMM2), and "Ground, Ford 5-1, request start" gets "start at your
discretion". A Vacuum call over your route is heard on STRIKE. No red tanker on any preset;
no AI chatter on Vacuum, Ground or Tower; on STRIKE only your own package's AI.

**Fail signature:** the ABCCC never answers or calls the push (the plugin got no packages);
Vacuum never on STRIKE; Ground or Tower shows a VHF frequency, or a preset that will not
tune; an AI flight from outside your package talks on STRIKE, or your package's AI are not
on it; "say again" to a start call.
