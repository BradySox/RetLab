### B216 — A Tomcat flight carries its squadron's callsign in the mission · §62 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** any campaign with an F-14 squadron (Red Flag 81-2, a carrier campaign). Plan a new
Tomcat flight after updating, generate and fly it or watch it.

- The flight reads as its squadron's callsign in the ATO and on the kneeboard (VF-143:
  Dog 1-1; VF-41: FastEagle 1-1).
- The F10 map and the unit label show the same name.
- The mission loads with no callsign error in the Retribution log, for the fragged flight
  and for parked spare Tomcats.
- A second flight from the same squadron is numbered 2.
- AWACS and ATC still answer the flight. They are not expected to say the name.

**Fail signature:** `'Dog' is not in list` or another callsign error at generation; a
blank or garbled callsign in the mission; every aircraft of that country picking up the
Tomcat callsign.
