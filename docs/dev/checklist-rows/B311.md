### B311 — F-4E inboard racks and Sidewinder rails never share a wing · §8 · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-11-05):** the payload editor empties a Sidewinder rail when an inboard F-4E store blocks it, and the reverse; the mission build empties any clashing rail left in a save and logs `station N emptied`. See `docs/dev/retlab-features.md` §8, Loadouts and module data.

**Pass:** on an F-4E flight, tick Use custom loadout, put AIM-9s on stations 2/4/10/12, then pick Mk-83 x3 (TER) on station 3: stations 2 and 4 turn to None at once. Pick an AIM-9 on station 4 again: station 3 turns to None. In DCS the wingmen carry every bomb the payload screen shows. An SWA load (2x Mk-82 SWA) on 3 keeps the AIM-9s.

**Fail signature:** an AIM-9 stays beside an inboard TER on the payload screen; a jet spawns with fewer bombs than the screen showed; an SWA load or a §71 AGM-78 fit loses its AIM-9s.
