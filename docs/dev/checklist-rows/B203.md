### B203 — Fog and a dust storm set in the Time & Weather window show up in DCS · §47 Time & Weather window · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** in Settings, untick **Use DCS' automatic fog setting**. Open the Time &
Weather window, tick **Fog**, set 0.5 NM visibility and 800 ft thickness, ACCEPT and
generate. In DCS the fields sit in fog with roughly half a mile visibility, and the
mission editor's weather tab shows fog in Manual mode. Do it again with **Dust storm**
at 0.5 NM instead: the mission has a dust storm. Leave the automatic fog setting on
and the two boxes are greyed out.

**Fail signature:** clear air at the field with fog ticked; the mission editor shows
fog Off; the dust storm is missing with no fog in the mission.
