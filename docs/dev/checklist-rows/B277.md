### B277 — Take off opens no extra windows: mission status in the map's place, SkyEye hidden · §8 + §120 · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-24):** the "Waiting for mission completion" window is gone; its
losses table and buttons fill the map area while the mission runs. SkyEye's console is
hidden (was minimized) and its log opens from a **SkyEye log** button. The SRS server
starts minimized.

**Pass:** with Voice bots on and SRS, SkyEye and the SRS client closed, press Take off. No
new RetLab window opens: the map area shows the status and the buttons, and the menu,
toolbar, flight list and top bar are greyed out. The taskbar shows no SkyEye console; the
SRS server is minimized (not in front); the SRS client opens as before. Press **SkyEye
log**: SkyEye's output shows and grows. Fly and finish, press **Accept results**: the
debrief window opens, the map comes back with the new turn, and the controls work again.
In the SRS server's client list "GCI ... [BOT]" drops at once (SkyEye signed off). Also
press Take off and then **Abort mission**: the map comes back and planning works.

**Fail signature:** a SkyEye window on the taskbar; the SRS server opens in front (its
window ignores the minimized start); the map stays on the status after Accept or Abort;
the controls stay greyed out; SkyEye survives the debrief, or RetLab's log says "closing
it by force" every time (the hidden console no longer takes the close request).
