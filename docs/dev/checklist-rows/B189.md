### B189 — A flight ticked "Refuel before the push" tanks between Hold and Join · tanker box · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** in any campaign with a theater tanker, open a strike or SEAD flight's
Waypoints tab and tick **Refuel before the push**. The list reads Hold, Refuel, Join,
the Hold depart time moves earlier and the TOT does not move. Fly it, or watch an AI
flight in Tacview: the flight reaches the tanker box, plugs in (an AI flight fills to
about 90%), and still makes the Join on time. A jet that gets home on that top-off has
no Refuel after the Split; one that cannot keeps both.

**Fail signature:** an AI flight flies through the tanker box without plugging in; the
flight is late to the Join by about the tanking time; the Refuel point sits on a
package tanker's track (that tanker is not up before the strike); the box can be
ticked when no theater tanker can refuel the jet.
