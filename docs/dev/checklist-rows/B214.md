### B214 — Clean on a pylon that cannot be removed does no harm in DCS · §8 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** any player jet. In the loadout editor pick **Clean** on one pylon that has a
removable rack (an F-16 or Hornet wing station) and on one that does not, then generate.

- The mission loads with no payload error for that flight in `dcs.log`.
- The removable station shows bare, with no rack.
- The other station shows as it does with nothing loaded.
- The rearm window opens and both stations can be loaded again.

**Fail signature:** the mission refusing to load; the aircraft spawning with a missing
or wrong store on a neighbouring station; a rearm window that will not open.
