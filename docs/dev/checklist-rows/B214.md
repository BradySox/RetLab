### B214 — Clean (pylon removed) takes the rack off in DCS · §8 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** any player jet with removable racks (an F-16 or Hornet wing station). In the
loadout editor pick **Clean (pylon removed)** on one wing station, then generate.

- The mission loads with no payload error for that flight in `dcs.log`.
- The station shows bare, with no rack.
- The rearm window opens and the station can be loaded again.
- On an F-4E the Sparrow wells (stations 5, 6, 8) list None but no Clean.

**Fail signature:** the mission refusing to load; the rack still fitted; the aircraft
spawning with a missing or wrong store on a neighbouring station; a rearm window that
will not open.
