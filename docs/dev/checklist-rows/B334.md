### B334 — Every flight's route bends around known SAM rings, and the package window names the rest · §69 route around SAMs · ☐ UNTESTED

Built 2026-11-12. Unit-tested (`tests/flightplan/test_samdetour.py`); never flown.

**Setup:** a new game (the setting is on) with known enemy SAM sites between your bases and the
front, such as Syria - The Long Road to H3. Pass a turn and open the map.

**Pass:** a BARCAP's, tanker's or escort's line to and from its station bends around the edge
of a known SAM ring it does not need to enter, where the way round is no more than half again
as long. A site you have not found has no bend round it. A helicopter flies straight. Open a
package whose attack run or racetrack still sits in a ring: the red warning line names the
flight and the site. Flown: the SAM does not engage the flight on the bent legs.

**Fail signature:** a transit leg straight through a known ring with an easy way round; a bend
round a site the map does not show; a warning naming a ring the route only touches at its
target; the turn takes much longer to plan than before.
