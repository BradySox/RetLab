### B290 — Mission Info shows QNH, surface wind and a Brg column; dimmer paper · Kneeboard (Mission Info, all daytime pages) · ☐ UNTESTED

Generate a mission with the recon kneeboard pages on and take a ground-start player flight from a land airfield. Open its Mission Info page in the cockpit.

**Pass:** under the bullseye line, a `QNH: xx.xx inHg (xxxx hPa)` line, a QFE line for the departure field, and a `Surface wind: xxx° / xxkts` line. The QNH matches the departure field's ATIS and the Departure page. The flight plan has a `Brg` column between `Dist` and `GS`, with three-digit true courses that match the legs on the F10 map. Nothing in the flight plan table wraps or runs off the right edge.

**Fail signature:** no QNH on Mission Info; a QNH that differs from the field's ATIS; a Brg value off by roughly the magnetic variation from the F10 ruler's true course; a waypoint name wrapping to a second row or a cut-off Fuel column.

**Also check (paper tone):** in daylight the text pages are mid grey and the map pages (Departure, Overview, Target) a light grey, not white. Colored text (blue frequencies, amber bingo/joker, red threats) reads without leaning in. **Fail:** a page still near white, or colored text hard to read on the darker paper.
