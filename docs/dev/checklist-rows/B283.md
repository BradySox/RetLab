### B283 — Modern FOB walls, FARP props and the stock CurrentHill units · modern FOB · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** on a new game with a 2000-or-later faction that owns a FOB (USA 2020, Russia 2020),
the FOB shows a closed HESCO wall with a watchtower at each corner and a gap with a boom
barrier on one side, with the old FOB's command center, barracks and garages inside. Tents
and a generator stand beside the FOB's first helipad, clear of every pad. A Tiger UHT
squadron (Blufor current, NATO Baltic 2027) flies CAS with HOT 3 and rockets. BMP-3 ERA,
Titan, Dragoon and the HX77, HX81 and SLT-50 trucks appear on the front or in convoys.

**Fail signature:** wall segments standing across the wall line instead of along it; a gap
between segments wide enough to drive through other than the gate; a prop overlapping a
building, a pad or a parked helicopter; a Tiger UHT that takes off with no weapons; a 1990 or
older faction showing the HESCO FOB.
