### B324 — F-16 and F/A-18 presets in order of flight · comm card · ☐ UNTESTED

Generate any mission with a player F-16 flight and a player F/A-18 flight in one package.
In each cockpit, step through the presets and compare them with the Comm Card kneeboard page.

**Pass:**
- F-16 COM1: Ground, Tower, Departure, AWACS, STRIKE, Vacuum (when it flies), tankers,
  Approach, in that order. COM2 Ch 1 is the flight, Ch 2 the Hornets.
- F/A-18 COMM1 Ch 1 is the flight, then the same order from Ch 2. COMM2 Ch 1 is the flight,
  Ch 2 the Vipers.
- Every preset in the cockpit matches its row on the Comm Card.

**Fail signature:** a preset in the cockpit that differs from the card; the Hornet's own
flight missing from COMM1 Ch 1; an F-16 COM2 channel the ARC-222 cannot tune.
