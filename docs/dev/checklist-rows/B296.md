### B296 — A damaged ship starts the next mission damaged · §124 · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-29):** with **Ships keep their damage between turns** on (the default), a ship's health is saved at debrief, the next mission spawns it at that health through DCS's "UNIT SET LIFE %" trigger action, and each turn repairs 34 points. See `docs/dev/retlab-features.md` §124.

**Pass:** hit an enemy or friendly warship without sinking it and end the mission. The ship's group window shows **Health NN%**. In the next mission, the same ship takes noticeably fewer hits to sink (or, in Tacview or the F10 info bar, shows damage from the start). The following turn its window shows the health 34 points higher.

**Fail signature:** the window shows the health but the ship spawns undamaged (the native action did not apply: check `dcs.log` for the trigger); a damaged ship that sinks or burns at mission start; a carrier at low health that stops launching or recovering aircraft (note it: a known unknown, not a fault of the save).
