### B232 — Sectors decide the ground war · §111 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** a new game (Red Tide or Abchazian Blitz) with *Sectors decide the ground war* on.
Fly or fast-forward a few turns with an aggressive stance on one front.

- After a won front line battle, the turn's messages name the sectors taken, and the
  territory fill on the F10 map and the app map grows by those sectors.
- The front line sits on the new border, where the road between the two bases enters the
  enemy's color.
- A base whose sector is taken changes hands at the end of the turn, with a "captured" or
  "lost" message, and its front lines move to the next base.
- An Air Assault capture in the mission still takes the base, and its sector turns with it.
- A road between two of your bases that the enemy's color crosses gets no convoys.
- A save from before this change loads with the setting off and its fronts where they were.

**Fail signature:** sectors taken far from the front or behind it, leaving a pocket; the
front line drawn inside one side's color; a base whose sector is taken staying put, or
flipping back next turn; the same base captured twice in one turn; convoys driving through
enemy sectors.
