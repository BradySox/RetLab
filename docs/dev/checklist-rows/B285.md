### B285 — A train runs its rail line and a dead train costs its cargo · §123 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Setup:** Fulda Gap 1985, any turn. Find a train on the map (a rail line between two
same-side bases with an active transport). Generate and fly.

**Pass:** the train spawns on the track at its first station and runs toward the other
base at about 30 mph. Destroy it: dcs.log shows `Train destroyed: '<name>' (<n> cargo
units)`, and the debrief lists every unit it carried as lost in a convoy.

**Fail signature:** the train sits still at spawn, runs the wrong way onto another line,
or dies with no `Train destroyed` line and its cargo arrives at the next turn.
