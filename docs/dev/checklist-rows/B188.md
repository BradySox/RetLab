### B188 — An LHA beside a carrier gets no BARCAP of its own · §6 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Try:** any campaign with a carrier and an LHA steaming together (Long Road to H3). Pass a turn and read the ATO.

**Pass:** BARCAP packages target the carrier only; none targets the LHA. An LHA more than 80 NM from any friendly carrier still gets its own BARCAP.

**Fail signature:** a BARCAP package with the LHA as its target while a carrier sits within 80 NM, or a lone LHA with no BARCAP at all.
