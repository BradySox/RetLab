### B191 — A CAP ticked "Refuel before station" tanks on its way out · tanker box · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** in any campaign with a theater tanker, open a BARCAP or TARCAP flight's
Waypoints tab and tick **Refuel before station**. The list reads Takeoff, Refuel,
Race-track start; takeoff moves earlier and the race-track times do not move. Fly it,
or watch an AI flight in Tacview: the flight plugs in at the tanker box (an AI flight
fills to about 90%) and reaches Race-track start on time.

**Fail signature:** an AI flight flies past the tanker without plugging in; the flight
is late on station by about the tanking time; the box can be ticked when no theater
tanker can refuel the jet.
