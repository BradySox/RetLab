### B294 — SA-2 and SA-3 sites hold fire until a target is well inside · Skynet IADS, unit data · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-26):** the Fan Song, Low Blow and Flat Face carry `go_live_range_in_percent: 70`. Skynet keeps an SA-2 or SA-3 dark until a target is within 70% of its missile range, and the launchers only fire inside that.

Test 65: between 09:23 and 09:40, MANDRILL fired all 18 of its SA-2s in pairs at nine flights skirting its ring. Most were edge shots that missed. When the DEAD package arrived at 09:46 it was out of missiles and dark, so the Tornados had nothing to shoot.

**Pass:** on a flight against a red SA-2 or SA-3 with flights passing at its edge, Tacview shows launches only at targets well inside its ring (at medium altitude, about 21 NM or less for an SA-2 and about 10 NM or less for an SA-3). The site still has missiles when the strike or DEAD package arrives.

**Fail signature:** a site that never fires at a jet flying straight over it; an SA-2 still firing pairs at targets 25 NM or more out; a Lua error in dcs.log naming `setGoLiveRangeInPercent`.
