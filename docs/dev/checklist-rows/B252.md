### B252 — Briefing screen shows the per-mission card and clean text · §107 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** Load a generated mission with no `briefing.png` in `Saved Games\DCS\Retribution`.
The briefing screen picture shows the campaign name, turn, date and start time, weather and
your flights, readable at full screen. The text reads `AEW&C`, each flight line starts with
its callsign, and the ally lines say `target:`.

**Fail signature:** The old F-16 picture or no picture (check `dcs.log` and the
Retribution log for "Briefing card failed"), clipped or unreadable text on the card, or
`&amp;` in the briefing text.
