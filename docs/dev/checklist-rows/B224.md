### B224 — A Mirage F1 on OCA/Aircraft carries its Belouga cluster bombs · Mirage F1 presets · ☐ UNTESTED

Built 2026-10-11 during the loadout pass after test 55. Eight Mirage F1 preset files named
`{BLG66_BELOUGA}`, which is not a DCS store (Aerges declares the Belouga as `{BLG66_AC}`),
so the DEAD and OCA/Aircraft presets were dropped whole and the jet flew a fallback fit.
The 64 entries now carry `{BLG66_AC}`. Unit-tested (`tests/retlab/test_mirage_f1_belouga.py`);
never looked at in DCS.

**Pass:** any campaign with a Mirage F1 squadron (F1CE, F1EE, F1BE, F1M and the rest). Frag
or let the planner frag an OCA/Aircraft flight.

- The Payload tab shows *Retribution OCA/Aircraft* with four BLG-66 Belouga, two Magics and
  the centerline tank.
- In DCS the jet has the four cluster bombs on the wing pylons and releases them on the
  parked aircraft.
- No payload error for the flight in `dcs.log`.

**Fail signature:** the flight on a different fit than the preset names; empty wing pylons;
a `cannot equip` line for the flight in the Retribution log.
