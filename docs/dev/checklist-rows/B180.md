### B180 — A captured base's motorpool depot changes hands · §56 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** in Red Tide, capture Haina (or load a save after Haina has fallen). The Haina
depot marker turns blue and belongs to whichever land base or FARP is now nearest it; its
parked vehicles in the next mission are that base's reserve, not red's. Loading an older
save keeps exactly one depot at Haina, not two.

**Fail signature:** the depot stays red after the capture; a blue base's depot is
populated with red armor; or a second depot marker appears at the same spot after a load.
