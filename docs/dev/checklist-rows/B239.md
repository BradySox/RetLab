### B239 — An AI twin waits for a late-spawning player group and takes the package's escorts · §113 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** single player, one player flight that is the primary of a package with an AI
Escort or SEAD Escort flight, *AI fills if no player* ticked, takeoff 20 or more minutes
after mission start (the client group is late-activated).

- Slot in when the client group appears and fly it: no "has no players" message, and no
  `AI ...` group ever appears.
- Re-fly with the slot left empty: the message comes 5 minutes after the client group's
  planned spawn, the twin appears then, and the escort flight, once airborne, joins and
  flies with the twin to the target. dcs.log has `AI fill: <escort> escorts the twin`.
- At the split the escort leaves the twin and flies the rest of its route home.
- Note whether the package's split trigger fires early with the client slot empty (an
  unoccupied client group may read as dead): escorts turning for home at once is the sign.

**Fail signature:** the twin launches while the player flies the client group; the escort
holds or orbits away from the twin, or flies straight home; `AI fill:` errors in dcs.log.
