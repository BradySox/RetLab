### B336 — Threat reaction follows a late lock and a retarget · §94 · ☐ UNTESTED

Any mission with Smart threat reaction on where a SAM or fighter fires at AI flights, ideally
two flights close together. Record Tacview.

**Pass:**
- `dcs.log` has `AIReaction| Selective missile tracking loaded` and, after shots,
  `COMMAND_ACCEPTED ... option=EVADE` for the targeted group, then `option=PASSIVE` once the
  missile is gone. A once-a-minute `STATS` line appears.
- Only the flight the missile is guiding on breaks; nearby flights hold their route.
- A missile that switches to another flight logs `RETARGET`, and the new flight breaks while
  the old one returns to its route (unless another missile is still on it).
- §61 scrambled bandits still break on their own shots.
- No stutter during an anti-ship or cruise-missile salvo.

**Fail signature:** a targeted flight flies straight into a missile that resolved its target
(look for `OPTION_FAILED` repeating); every flight breaks; a flight stays defensive after its
missile is gone; a frame hitch during a salvo; scrambled bandits never break.
