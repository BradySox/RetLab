### B236 — A hand-planned strike lands behind its SEAD · Coordinated TOT (§114) · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** plan a SEAD or DEAD package on a SAM, then a Strike or BAI package from the map on a
target inside that SAM's ring. Its window shows **Coordinated** ticked and a TOT 2 to 8
minutes after the SEAD package's, with "behind the SEAD on <site>". Add or move the SEAD
package after closing the strike's window, press Take Off: the strike's TOT on the kneeboard
follows the new SEAD time. In the mission, the SEAD flight reaches the SAM before the strikers
reach the target. A strike on a target under a known SAM with no SEAD planned shows a red
warning line naming that SAM and keeps its ASAP time.

**Fail signature:** the strike's TOT is ahead of the SEAD's; a TOT that does not change at
Take Off after the SEAD package moved; a warning naming a site the map does not show; the
package window will not open or errors on Add Flight.
