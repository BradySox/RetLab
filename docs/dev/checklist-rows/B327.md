### B327 — A mission leaves out the scripts with nothing to do · §8 · ☐ UNTESTED

Generate two missions: one with a carrier, a helicopter Transport flight and CSAR on, and one
with none of these (a land campaign, CSAR off on both sides, no Transport or Air Assault
flight). Fly a few minutes of each.

**Pass:**
- The second mission's Retribution log has a `Plugin airboss left out`, `Plugin ctld left out`
  and `Plugin opscsar left out` line; the first has none of these.
- In the first mission the carrier answers on Airboss frequencies, the transport pilot has the
  CTLD F10 menu, and an ejected pilot gets a CSAR beacon.
- `dcs.log` for both missions has no Lua error naming `ctld`, `AIRBOSS`, `CSAR` or a nil
  global from another plugin.

**Fail signature:** a feature that should run is silent and its plugin is named in a
`left out` line; a `dcs.log` error about a missing global from a left-out script.
