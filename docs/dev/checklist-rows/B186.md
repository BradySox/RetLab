### B186 — An auto cruise raid never flies past a point-defended SAM · §63 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** in Iron Gate with cruise missile auto raids on, pass a turn and fly or watch
it. The Tomahawk salvo's straight line from the Burke to its target stays outside the
ring of every red SAM site that has point defense (the SA-10 at Mineralnye Vody and
every other site with a PD group). In Tacview no SA-10 or SA-6 missile is fired at a
Tomahawk. If every target is behind such a SAM, no raid launches and the Burke's
magazine is unchanged next turn.

**Fail signature:** a salvo whose line crosses a point-defended SAM's ring; the SA-10
firing at Tomahawks before the strike package arrives (test 54: 22 of 24 missiles).
