### B215 — A player calls a ballistic missile strike onto an F10 marker and the round lands · §110 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** tick **Ballistic missile strikes** in Plugin Options. Play a faction that fields a
ballistic launcher (a Scud-B, or a CurrentHill Iskander or ATACMS) with a missile site on
your side. Put an F10 marker on a target 60 to 150 NM from the launcher and type a name
on it. Fly it in a two-ship with another pilot if you can, on a server.

- F10 > Other has **Ballistic Missile Strike**, with your battery and its round count.
- *Refresh target list*, then battery > the marker > *1 round(s)*: the battery answers
  "fire mission acknowledged" with the range in NM. The marker is listed by the name you
  typed, and markers other scripts drew (tanker and CAP stations) are not listed.
- In a two-ship there is one **Ballistic Missile Strike** menu, and it still works for
  the pilot who stays after the other leaves the slot.
- A round leaves the launcher within about 12 minutes, "shot" is called, and "splash" is
  called when it arrives. The round lands on the marker.
- *Battery status* shows one round fewer and "reloading".
- A marker 20 NM from an Iskander is refused with "inside minimum range".
- The enemy's launchers are not on your menu, and red's missile sites still fire as before.
- No `bmstrike` or `Ballistic Missile Strike` script error in `dcs.log`.

**Fail signature:** no menu; your own marker missing from the target list; two
**Ballistic Missile Strike** menus; "rounds complete" for a launcher that was destroyed
before it fired; "unable to fire" on a target that is in range; a round that
launches and lands far from the marker; the mission's other F10 menus disappearing; a
launcher that fires at its pre-planned target instead of the marker.
