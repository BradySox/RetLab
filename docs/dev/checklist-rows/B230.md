### B230 — Front line battles spread along the border · §111 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** Red Tide or Iraq Desert Storm, a turn with ground units on the front. Generate
and open the mission.

- The front's units stand in several groups along the border between the two colors, one
  fight per sector, instead of on one short line across the road.
- Each fight has both sides in it, facing each other across the border.
- The F10 front line runs along the border from the first fight to the last.
- The JTAC orbits one of the fights and finds targets.
- Unticking *Front line battles spread along the border* puts the units back on one line.

**Fail signature:** units on top of each other at one point; a group spawned in water,
forest or a town; groups facing away from the enemy or driving along the border instead of
across it; a fight with only one side in it; the JTAC orbiting empty ground.
