### B292 — Voice ATC military calls: taxi, takeoff, overhead, base · §119 · ☐ UNTESTED

Needs the `BradySox/skyeye` PR from branch `retlab/military-atc`. Fly a two-ship player
flight with Voice ATC on, Strict.

**Pass:** "Ground, Viper 1-1, flight of two, request taxi" gets "Viper 1-1 flight, runway 25,
altimeter 29.92, taxi, hold short runway 25, contact Tower ..."; with "have the numbers" no
altimeter, and the takeoff clearance then has no wind. "Holding short, ready" gets "runway 25,
wind ..., cleared for takeoff, frequency change approved", and no airborne call is asked for.
"Tower, Viper 1-1, airborne, switching departure" gets "frequency change approved", never a
second takeoff clearance. "10 miles, full stop" gets "runway 25, wind ..., report initial";
"request straight-in" gets "make straight-in, report 3-mile final". "Viper 1-1, initial" (no
station) gets "roger". "Viper 1-2, base, gear down" gets "Viper 1-2, runway 25, wind ...,
cleared to land", and the landing draws no "landed without clearance". "Tower, Viper 1-1,
engine failure" gets "cleared to land any runway". On Relaxed: "Tower, Viper 1-1, request
runway in use" gets "runway 25, wind ..."; "Viper 1, clear for takeoff 25" is taken as the
readback; a misheard flight number ("Viper 2" for Viper 1-1, the only Viper flight) is
answered as Viper 1-1, not "say again your callsign".

**Fail signature:** Tower clears to land on the inbound call; a pattern call without "Tower"
gets no answer; "flight of two" is read as ship 2; "brake" or "break" is heard as something
else; "engine failure" gets "start at your discretion"; a Relaxed readback with the altimeter
or wind in it gets "negative".
