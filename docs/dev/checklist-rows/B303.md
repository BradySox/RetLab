### B303 — A KS-19 site's Fire Can reports contacts to Skynet · Skynet return · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-11-02):** Skynet's database has a Fire Can entry (`SON_9`, plus the retired High Digit SAMs `Fire Can radar`). A KS-19 gun site goes to Skynet as an EWR named after its first live unit, the Fire Can; before this entry that EWR reported nothing. The guns still fire on their own. See `docs/dev/design/retlab-skynet-return-notes.md`, "Fixes from Juan's fork".

**Setup:** a campaign whose red faction fields the KS-19/SON-9 group (russia_1980, iraq_1991, nva_1970, warsaw_pact_central_front_1985 and others), with a KS-19 site on the map. Turn on the Skynet plugin's red debug option.

**Pass:** in `dcs.log`, Skynet lists the site's Fire Can as an EW radar of type Fire Can, and while a blue jet is within about 19 NM of it the red IADS status (F10 menu) shows the Fire Can with contacts.

**Fail signature:** the Fire Can is listed as an EW radar with no type, or with no contacts while a jet is in its range; or Skynet logs "you have added an EW Radar that does not exist" for the site.
