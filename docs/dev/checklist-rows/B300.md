### B300 — A player flight lines up on final and holds straight out the runway · §126 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Setup:** any airfield campaign with surface wind of 12 kt or more, plus one carrier
campaign. Tick a player seat on a strike flight after the planner has built it. Generate
and fly.

**Pass:** the flight's HOLD sits straight out the runway you took off from, 25 NM out
(modern doctrine). On the way home ALIGN is 15 NM out on final for the runway the
kneeboard names first, about 4,500 ft above the field, and flying to it puts you on the
ILS. On a carrier, ALIGN is 50 NM astern on the angled-deck final bearing at 6,000 ft.
AI flights in the same mission have neither.

**Fail signature:** ALIGN sits off to one side of the extended centerline, on the other
runway end in a 12 kt+ wind, or across the deck; the hold is back at the usual spot
although no enemy threat ring covers the runway point; an AI flight's route shows ALIGN.
