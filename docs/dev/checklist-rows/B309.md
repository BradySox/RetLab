### B309 — F-4E AUX radio carries listen copies of flight, AWACS, Vacuum, STRIKE · §8 comm card · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** In an F-4E with Voice AWACS on, Function selector on T/R+G ADF or ADF+G CMD, AUX Ch 1 is the flight, Ch 2 SkyEye, Ch 3 Vacuum, Ch 4 STRIKE (when an ABCCC is fragged), as the comm card's AUX column says. A SkyEye or Vacuum call is heard on AUX while COMM is on Tower. AUX Ch 5-20 repeat Ch 1.

**Fail signature:** AUX channels carry the module's default frequencies, or no audio is heard on AUX through SRS, or the card's AUX column does not match the cockpit.
