### B307 — Approach clears an ILS in low weather or on request · §119 step 3 · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-11-03):** Approach can clear an ILS, TACAN or LOC approach from the Kola, Falklands and Nevada plates, or a generated 12 NM straight-in at any other ILS runway. It does so in low weather or when the pilot asks; Tower then clears the flight to land. See `docs/dev/retlab-features.md` §119.

**Pass:** with Voice ATC on, inbound in clear weather, say "request ILS" on Approach: you get "cleared ILS runway N", a start fix or a 12 mile final, an altitude, and where to call Tower. On a Kola field (Bodo 25) the fix is TAKUP at 3,500. Call Tower at the final fix: "cleared to land". In a mission with overcast under 3,000 ft, the inbound call alone gets the ILS. The ATC card lists the approaches.

**Fail signature:** "request ILS" gets the visual entry or "say again"; Tower answers "report initial" after an ILS clearance; the ILS frequency on the card is not what the jet's ILS receives.
