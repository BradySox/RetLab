### B227 — An alert jet launches with its squadron's CAP loadout · §1 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** any campaign where an alert (QRA) squadron scrambles. Operation Desert Aladeen
turn 1 (test 56) has an F-15E and an F-22 alert pair on blue and JF-17, MiG-29, MiG-21
and Mirage F1 alert jets on red. Watch a scramble in the F2 view or Tacview.

- A scrambled jet carries the same missiles as a BARCAP flight of its type that turn.
- With Restrict weapons by date on, it carries nothing newer than the campaign date.
- The F-15E alert pair carries no bombs and no captive Sidewinder.
- A Mirage F1 alert jet has missiles on its pylons.
- A host-scramble jet from the F10 menu (§61) is armed the same way.

**Fail signature:** an alert jet with empty pylons, practice bombs or one missile; an
alert jet carrying a weapon the date limit removed from the ATO flights of the same type.
