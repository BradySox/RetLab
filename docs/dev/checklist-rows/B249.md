### B249 — Heatblur jets spawn at the period Aircraft Condition · §43 · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-18):** a player F-4E or A-6E whose Aircraft Condition was left alone
spawns at 90, or 75 when the mission date is before 1980, instead of Heatblur's showroom 100.
A value set in the payload tab or saved as a default wins. Unit tests pin what the generator
writes; what DCS does with it is not tested.

**Pass:** a player F-4E flight in any campaign.

- The payload tab shows Aircraft Condition at 90 (75 in a pre-1980 campaign) without touching it.
- In the Mission Editor, the generated `.miz` shows the same value on the player F-4E.
- Setting it to 100 in the payload tab gives 100 in the `.miz`.
- An AI F-4E's properties are unchanged.

**Fail signature:** the `.miz` still reads 100 on an untouched player jet; switching flight
members in the payload tab pins a value onto a member you never edited.
