### B297 — A picked missile-site target is hit dead center · §125 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Setup:** a campaign with a blue Scud site (Generate fire tasks for missile sites on). Open the site's window, pick an enemy SAM site or base in **Missile target**, generate.

**Pass:** in the mission (Tacview), every launcher at the site fires once at the picked target and the missiles land on it, within the Scud's own spread. Next turn the window still shows the pick; once the target is destroyed and the turn passes, the box is back on **Random enemy base**. A command post you have not found is never in the list.

**Fail signature:** the site fires at a random base with a pick set; the missiles aim up to 1.5 miles off the pick; the pick is gone the next turn with the target still alive; a hidden command post appears in the list.
