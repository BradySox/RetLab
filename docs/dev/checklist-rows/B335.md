### B335 — QRA scrambles on EASYGCICAP, low, fast and clear of the field · §1 · ☐ UNTESTED

Any campaign turn where the enemy has alert jets and you fly a Strike package into their
reach. Record Tacview. Note the base each scramble comes from.

**Pass:**
- `dcs.log` has `Intercept: configuring QRA` and no `DETECTION_MANAGER` or `EASYGCICAP`
  error; each alert base logs no `no anchor static` line.
- Alert jets appear airborne over their own base about 2,500 ft above it at about 300 kt
  and climb away; two launched together are about 500 ft apart.
- A 4-ship strike draws at most 4 jets; a fighter sweep alone draws none.
- The F10 map shows no container at the field; in the 3D world the container sits off the
  runway axis and the parking, at least 1.1 mi from the field center.
- AI taxi and takeoff at those bases are normal.
- The debrief books lost alert jets against their squadron; jets that land are not lost.

**Fail signature:** no scramble at all (look for an `EASYGCICAP` or `_AssignIntercept` error);
jets spawned in the hills, stalling, or overlapping; more than 4 jets on one raid; a scramble
against a sweep; AI stuck taxiing near the container; survivors wrong in the debrief.
