### B192 — The Hornet SA page shows the borders, the attack lane and the TACAN list · §74 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** in a client Hornet with the cartridge loaded, on any campaign whose route passes within 40 NM of a land border: the SA page shows the nearest border as a dashed line and the next border as a solid line. A CAS flight shows the front line as the solid line instead. A strike or SEAD flight shows its lane from the IP over the target to the split, as two parallel lines. The TACAN station list on the DTC page lists the boat, the home, arrival and divert fields and then the map's other TACANs nearest the route (10 at most), and selecting one puts its symbol where the field or boat actually is. With no border near the route, the dashed line is the tanker box, as before.

**Fail signature:** no dashed or solid line where a border is near the route; a front line on a non-CAS flight; no lane on a flight with an IP; a TACAN station drawn far from its field or boat; or the TACAN list empty after loading the cartridge.
