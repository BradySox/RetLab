### B267 — The map frees its video memory while the mission runs · §8 · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-21):** Take off hides the campaign map behind a "map is paused"
label until the mission window closes (Accept results, Abort or the close button). The
page stays loaded, hidden, so Chromium frees its tiles. The saving is unmeasured.

**Pass:** open Task Manager, Details tab, and add the "Dedicated GPU memory" column. Note it
for `python.exe` and every `QtWebEngineProcess.exe` with the map open, then after Take off
with DCS in the mission, then after Accept results. During the mission the total for those
processes is clearly lower than before Take off (record the numbers in this row). After
debrief the map is back at the same pan and zoom, with the new turn's state on it.
Abort mission also brings the map back.

**Fail signature:** dedicated GPU memory for `python.exe` and `QtWebEngineProcess.exe` does
not drop during the mission (hiding is not enough: next step is discarding the page with a
client view restore); the map stays on the paused label after the window closes; the map
shows the previous turn after debrief; the main window jumps or the info panel resizes.
