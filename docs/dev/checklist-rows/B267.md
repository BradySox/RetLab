### B267 — The map is closed while you fly and draws on the video card otherwise · §8 · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-22):** software rendering (#1356) is reverted, so the map draws on
the GPU again. On Take off the map's web view is destroyed and the mission status shows in
its place (row B277);
a new map is built when the mission ends (Accept results or Abort).

**Measured 2026-10-24 (no DCS):** Task Manager's per-process "Dedicated GPU memory" for
`python.exe` is not the app's share of the card. It read 3.1 GB at start, 11.8 GB after
New Game and 20 GB after one Take off and Abort, above the card's 16 GB, while the whole
card read 4.4 to 5.2 GB. Closing the app freed 0.6 GB. Closing the map at Take off freed
nothing measurable. The "4 GB" (#1354) and "13 GB" readings were this counter.

**How to measure:** `\GPU Adapter Memory(*)\Dedicated Usage` summed over adapters
(`typeperf` or `Get-Counter`), read with the app open, then a minute after closing it. The
drop is the app's share.

**Pass:** the map pans and zooms smoothly. During a mission the mission status shows in its place;
after debrief it comes back and shows the new turn. The app's share by the method above stays
under ~1 GB.

**Fail signature:** the map stays on the mission status after Accept results or Abort; the rebuilt
map is blank; the app's share by the method above is GBs.
