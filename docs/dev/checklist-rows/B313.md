### B313 — Real SAM sites swapped into 15 Syria campaigns · §122 · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-11-06):** in every Syria campaign but Anatolian Reach, Aegean Aegis and Blackball, a SAM or EWR marker with a real site of the same kind within 10 NM became that site, drawn unit by unit from the Levant template (140 in all; Full Map and Long Road to H3 19 each, Allied Sword 16). Eight factions gained support radars (Hawk CWAR, domed FPS-117, Roland radar). See `docs/dev/design/retlab-real-sam-sites-notes.md`.

**Pass:** start a new game of Full Map or Assault on Damascus. The New Game log has no `FIXED site ... cannot field` and no `Airfield clearance:` line. In DCS the swapped sites sit in the map's revetments; the campaign's SAM count and coverage look like before.

**Fail signature:** a site comes out as a different system than drawn, or for the wrong side; a launcher sits off its revetment; AI aircraft hold at a field near a drawn site.
