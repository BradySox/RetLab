### B223 — A Hind carries its anti-tank missiles on all four missile stations · Mi-24P presets · ☐ UNTESTED

Built 2026-10-11 from test 55 (`Pylon 2 cannot equip 2 x 9M120 Ataka` at Take Off). The
Mi-24P presets put the wingtip store `{2x9M120_Ataka_V}` on the outboard wing pylons (2
and 5). DCS lists `{2x9M120_Ataka_V_with_adapter}` for those pylons
(`CoreMods/aircraft/Mi-24P/Mi-24P.lua`, `outboard`) and has no table that swaps one for
the other. The presets now use the adapter store there. Unit-tested
(`tests/retlab/test_mi24p_loadouts.py`); never looked at in DCS.

**Pass:** any campaign where the AI or a player flies a Mi-24P on CAS, BAI, Escort or
OCA/Aircraft (red in Vectron's Claw flies the Escort).

- On the ramp or in the F2 view the Hind has missile tubes on both wingtip rails and on
  both outboard wing pylons.
- The rearm window shows 9M120 Ataka on stations 1, 2, 5 and 6 (8 missiles).
- The Retribution log has no `cannot equip` line for the flight.

**Fail signature:** empty outboard pylons on a Hind fragged with ATGMs; a payload error for
the flight in `dcs.log`; the `cannot equip` line still logged for pylons 2 and 5.
