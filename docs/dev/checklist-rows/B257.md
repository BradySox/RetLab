### B257 — SkyEye talks, the E-3 stays quiet · §116 voice AWACS · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-20):** a RetLab Features setting, Voice AWACS on SRS (SkyEye), off
by default. Blue AEW&C flights get radio silence; the SkyEye frequency (251.0 AM by default)
is held back from every other flight; the kneeboard AEW&C table and the briefing list
"Sentinel (SkyEye)" first and mark each blue AWACS "(silent)".

**Pass:** tick the setting, generate, start SkyEye and fly. The E-3 makes no radio calls
(no check-in, no picture, no threat calls) and still flies its orbit and shows on the
datalink. No other flight's frequency is 251.0. The kneeboard support page and the briefing
show the SkyEye line. Also record: does the F10 radio menu still offer the AWACS, and does
it answer a bogey dope request?

**Fail signature:** the E-3 still talks; it leaves its orbit or drops off the datalink; a
package or tanker lands on 251.0; the kneeboard line is missing or wraps badly.
