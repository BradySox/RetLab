### B279 — A FIXED site generates exactly as drawn · §122 · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-24):** a campaign group named `FIXED ...` keeps every unit where it
was placed instead of becoming a marker for a stock layout.

**Pass:** in a campaign with a FIXED site (Fulda Gap 1985 has 27),
start a New Game, generate the first turn and open the mission in the Mission Editor.
Every radar, launcher and truck of the site sits where the campaign `.miz` has it, facing
the same way. The site's SHORAD or AAA guns are a separate `(PD)` group. In flight, the
site's search radar comes up and it engages like a stock site of that type. After it is
damaged and repaired, the units are back on the same spots.

**Fail signature:** the site comes out in a stock ring or line; units are rotated around
the first unit; the site is missing and the log says "no air-defense preset group matches
its units" or "cannot field"; the site never lights its radar (Skynet role wrong).
