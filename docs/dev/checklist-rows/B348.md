### B348 — Red attacks arrive in grouped waves · §69 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** on a turn where red plans 4 or more attack packages (Strike, BAI, CAS, DEAD, SEAD, Anti-ship, OCA), red's package list shows them in 2 or 3 groups, each within about 3 minutes, spread from red's earliest arrival to just before the mission ends. A DEAD or SEAD in a group is about 2 minutes ahead of the others. In Tacview the raids arrive in those groups. Red's AWACS, tankers and CAP keep their usual times; blue's timing is unchanged.

**Fail signature:** red's attack packages still arrive one by one just after their earliest time, or a raid lands after the mission ends, or a red strike arrives ahead of the DEAD in its wave.
