### B273 — Vacuum finds are known after the turn · §3, §118 · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-22):** a red site whose radar a Vacuum heard is fully known after the
turn (units and threat ring on the map), as if it had been engaged. The next turn's Vacuum
starts with that site asleep, at the fix it had: tight if it was within about 1 NM.

**Pass:** fly a turn with Vacuum and an un-engaged mobile SAM (SA-6 or SA-11) that turns its
radar on. Strike nothing near it.
- After the debrief, a message "ELINT: Vacuum located enemy radars" names the site.
- On the map the site shows its units and threat ring.
- Next turn with Vacuum: "Vacuum, Ford 9, checking in" before it radiates names it asleep,
  tight if Vacuum heard it for a minute or more last turn, else loose.

**Fail signature:** the site stays a bare marker after the debrief (`vacuum_fixes` missing
from `state.json`, or its unit name not in the unit map); a site Vacuum never heard is
revealed; next turn's Vacuum calls it "blank" or "loose" after a tight fix last turn.
