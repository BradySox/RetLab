### B209 — An A-10's route keeps its planned altitudes, and a saved point sits at the kneeboard's number · §102 · ☐ UNTESTED

Built 2026-10-10 from juanjux/dcs-escalation #506, #510, #511 and #517, flown on his
install and not here. The CDU file now holds the saved points only; DCS builds the route.
Unit-tested (`tests/test_a10cdu.py`). Design:
`docs/dev/design/retlab-my-aircraft-notes.md` §8.

- **Setup:** a player A-10C or A-10C II with a route of several waypoints. Map →
  crosshair button → click a spot → Save as waypoint (or My aircraft → Add), with an
  altitude typed in. Generate and sit in the jet.
- **Pass (route):** CDU waypoint 0 is the aircraft's own position, once. Waypoint N on
  the CDU is waypoint N on the F10 map and on the kneeboard. A cruise waypoint shows its
  planned altitude (for example 15,000 ft), not the ground height under it.
- **Pass (saved point):** the kneeboard's saved points page gives the point a number,
  one past the last route waypoint. The CDU has that point at that number, in the EXTRA
  flight plan, with the elevation typed for it.
- **Fail signatures:** `INIT POSIT` listed twice; every CDU number one above the F10
  map; cruise waypoints at ground elevation; the saved point showing `EL: *****`; the
  saved point missing or on the mission flight plan.
