### B269 — Players can talk to Vacuum · §118 Vacuum ELINT · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-21):** five calls to Vacuum through SkyEye (check in, emitters,
target status, mud, say again), answered by the `vacuum` plugin, and a "Talking to Vacuum"
kneeboard card for SEAD, SEAD Escort and DEAD players. Needs the SkyEye build with
`elintRequest` (BradySox/skyeye#9).

**Pass:** fly a SEAD flight on a turn with Vacuum, SkyEye on the mission link, two red SAMs
radiating. The kneeboard has the Vacuum card after the AWACS card; a Strike flight's does
not. On Vacuum's frequency:
- "Vacuum, Ford 9, checking in": "Ford 9, Vacuum, two emitters active, nearest SA-.., bullseye ...",
  nearest to you, bullseye matching the F10 map.
- "Vacuum, Ford 9, say emitters": the radiating radars nearest first, bearing and NM from your
  jet; the bearing matches your HSI within a few degrees.
- "Vacuum, Ford 9, request target status": the radars on near your package target, or
  "target area blank".
- "Vacuum, Ford 9, mud 270" with a spike on the RWR at 270: names that radar; a bearing with
  nothing on it gets "no emitter 270".
- "Vacuum, Ford 9, say again": repeats its last answer.
- "Ford 9, checking in" (no name) on Vacuum's frequency is answered by Vacuum; "Chainsaw,
  Ford 9, picture" on it is answered by the AWACS, not Vacuum.
Each answer also shows as text to your flight for 15 s.

**Fail signature:** no answer (SkyEye log "failed to pass ELINT call": no `tune` with a
callsign reached it); the GCI answers a Vacuum call; bearings off by the magnetic variation
(declination fell back to 0 with MOOSE's `UTILS` missing); "Station calling Vacuum, say again
your callsign" for a correct callsign (seat matching broken); a dark SAM reported.
