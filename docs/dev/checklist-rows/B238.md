### B238 — Package Targets Map: land past the map edge, national borders · §4 · ☐ UNTESTED

Generate Desert Aladeen (Iraq) and open the last kneeboard page in the cockpit.

**Pass:** The page's west and north edges are tan land, not sea. The Persian Gulf around Kharg is
still blue. Thin purple lines trace the Iraq, Syria, Jordan, Saudi, Iran and Turkey borders. On a
Caucasus or Syria page that uses the satellite picture, the borders still show.

**Fail signature:** A blue band down the west or north edge of an Iraq page, the Gulf drawn as
land, or a purple line along a coastline or the map's straight edge.
