### B226 — No red ambush team behind your lines while land convoys are disabled · §50 · ☐ UNTESTED

Built 2026-10-11 from an Iraq save (Operation Desert Aladeen, turn 1) with **Disable land
convoys** ticked. Three hidden ambush teams were seeded on the Baghdad to Al-Salam road for a
convoy that never spawned, and each was generated with no options: weapons-free MT-LBs and
riflemen 2.6 to 6.1 miles from blue bases. The load-time placement box named all three.
Unit-tested, and checked by generating missions from copies of three saves; never looked at
in DCS.

**Pass, convoys disabled:** any campaign with a road between two blue bases, **Disable land
convoys** ticked, **Friendly convoy ambushes** ticked. Pass a turn or two, tick the fog
reveal, and generate.

- No red vehicle group sits on a road between your own bases, on the map or in DCS.
- Loading the save shows no "Ground object placement problem" box naming a group you
  cannot see with the fog reveal off.

**Pass, convoys enabled:** the same campaign with **Disable land convoys** unticked.

- With the fog reveal on, a turn where the roll hits shows one to three red teams on a blue
  convoy road. In DCS each holds fire until the convoy reaches it, then the TROOPS IN CONTACT
  call fires.
- Untick **Friendly convoy ambushes** without passing the turn, generate, and the teams are
  not in the mission.

**Fail signature:** a red MT-LB or BTR team with riflemen near a blue base that fires at
aircraft or vehicles with no TROOPS IN CONTACT call; the placement box naming groups owned
by a red base hundreds of miles away.
