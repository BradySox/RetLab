### B178 — Loadout editor hides training and non-combat stores · §73 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** in Edit flight → Payload for an A-10C II with a custom loadout, **Show training
and non-combat stores** is unticked. A rocket pylon's dropdown lists M151 HE and the M156
(white phosphorus) rockets but not M257 IL, Mk 61 TP, WTU-1/B TP or Mk 1 HE; a TCTS pod
and BDU practice bombs are missing too. Ticking the box lists them all. Fit a BDU-33,
untick the box: the BDU-33 stays selected and listed. Toggling the box never changes the
fitted stores or the laser code set on a pylon.

**Fail signature:** a WP or M156 rocket missing while unticked; a fitted training store
that vanishes or changes when the box is toggled; the loadout or pylon settings change on
a toggle.
