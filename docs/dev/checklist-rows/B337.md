### B337 — Night flights get goggles; JHMCS jets keep their sight · §24 · ☐ UNTESTED

A turn whose mission starts at dusk or night, with an A-10C II, a Harrier or Strike Eagle, and
an F-16C or F/A-18C among the player flights.

**Pass:**
- The A-10C II spawns with the HMCS and goggles available (the goggle toggle works).
- The Harrier and Strike Eagle have goggles mounted.
- The F-16C and F/A-18C still have JHMCS; no goggles unless picked in the payload tab.
- A daytime mission changes none of these.

**Fail signature:** no goggles on a night A-10C II, Harrier or Strike Eagle; an F-16C or
Hornet that lost its JHMCS; goggles fitted on a midday flight.
