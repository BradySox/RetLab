### B272 — Vacuum awake and asleep, tight and loose · §118 Vacuum ELINT · ☐ UNTESTED

**What changed (2026-10-22):** Vacuum remembers every radar it has located. A call says
whether the radar is on now ("awake") or was found earlier and is off ("asleep"), and whether
the fix is within about 1 NM ("tight") or up to 15 NM off ("loose"). Listening adds up across
blinks. Sites blue already knows start asleep: fixed SAMs tight, the rest loose. Needs the
SkyEye build with `status` and `fix` on the emitter message.

**Pass:** fly a SEAD flight on a turn with Vacuum, SkyEye on the mission link, one fixed SAM
(SA-2/3/5/10) and one mobile SAM blue has never engaged.
- The mobile SAM's first call is "Vacuum, SA-6 awake, bullseye ..., loose." The bullseye is
  off from the F10 map spot by a few NM (more the farther it is from Vacuum).
- After it has been on for a minute or two in total, Vacuum calls it once more, "tight", and
  the bullseye matches the F10 map within about 1 NM.
- "Vacuum, Ford 9, checking in" before any SAM is on: "none awake, one asleep, nearest SA-..
  asleep, ..., tight" for the fixed SAM.
- "Say emitters" lists awake radars first, then asleep ones, each with tight or loose.
- "Mud <bearing>" on a radar that is off: "no emitter <bearing>".

**Fail signature:** "new emitter" calls (old SkyEye build); every first call tight (the error
never applied); a radar never goes tight while Skynet blinks it (listening not adding up); an
asleep radar called unprompted; an asleep radar named on a mud call; a destroyed site still
listed.
