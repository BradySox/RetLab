### B288 — Four-ships park side by side and taxi out without jamming · §8 Flight parking clusters · ☐ UNTESTED

Fly or watch a mission with several AI four-ships and two-ships leaving one busy field (Fulda Gap 1985 at Spangdahlem, or any field with more than four departing flights). Note each flight's stands in the ME or Tacview and watch the taxi.

**Pass:** every flight's jets sit on neighbouring stands, the lead on the stand nearest the departure runway end; each flight taxis out lead first with the wingmen falling in behind, and every flight takes off.

**Fail signature:** a wingman taxiing toward its lead against the taxi flow; two jets of one flight nose to nose; a flight frozen on the taxiway for the rest of the mission; a lead parked behind its wingmen and waiting for them.
