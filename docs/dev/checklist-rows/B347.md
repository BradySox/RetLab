### B347 — Land towers are silent, carriers are not · §132 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** with *Silence AI ATC* on, the F10 ATC menu call to a player's airfield tower gets no reply and no tower calls are heard. A carrier's tower still answers. AI flights take off and land as usual. With it off, towers talk as before.

**Fail signature:** a land tower still answers or makes calls, or the carrier goes silent, or AI flights hold on the ground. The DCS log line `RetLab silent ATC: N towers silenced` is missing or shows 0.
