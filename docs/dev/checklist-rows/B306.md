### B306 — A cluster bomb's bomblets score one hit per canister · §91, §108 · ☐ UNTESTED

Any sortie that drops Rockeye, CBU-87 or CBU-97 on something that dies (test 67's DEAD
BUFFALO: 42 Rockeyes, 13 of 13 destroyed, 0 hits).

**Pass:** the flight's report card shows hits above 0 and no more than its weapons released.
A flight that also fired missiles or guns shows no more hits than before for those.

**Fail signature:** still 0 hits after the target died (DCS does not report the bomblet as a
bomb, or the canister still exists when its bomblets land); or hits above weapons released.
