### B181 — A motorpool depot's tooltip lists its inventory and follows your orders · §56 · ☐ UNTESTED

**Pass:** in Red Tide a couple of turns in, hover the Haina depot on the map. The tooltip
lists Motorpool reserve, Expected next turn, Unrendered reserve and In transit. Buy armor at
a blue base with a depot, or order a transfer out of it: its tooltip changes without passing
the turn. The vehicles parked at the depot in the generated mission match the reserve list.
A red depot you have not engaged shows no reserve lists.

**Fail signature:** the lists are missing or always "None" with armor in the base; they only
change after a turn passes; the mission parks different vehicles; or a red depot shows its
reserve while its units are fogged.
