### B240 — AI CAS flies the battle line and works each battle · §111 · ☐ UNTESTED

With *Front line battles spread along the border* on, plan an AI CAS flight on a long front
(Desert Storm H-3, or any front with 4 or more battles). Its route reads FLOT START, BATTLE 1
to 3, FLOT END, each at a battle. Fly the mission or spectate the CAS group in F10.

**Pass:** The AI reaches FLOT START, attacks ground units at that battle, moves on to the next
stop and attacks there, and so on to FLOT END, then flies home. Every attack is on a battle
within the engagement range (10 NM stock) of a stop. Tacview shows the bombs and rockets
spread along the border, not all at one battle.

**Fail signature:** The AI never gets past the first battle, or works it and goes home with
stores left; it attacks units deep behind the line, beyond 10 NM of every stop; or it
overflies the stops without attacking the battles under them. Going back to an earlier
battle is expected: each stop's search circle stays on for the rest of the route.
