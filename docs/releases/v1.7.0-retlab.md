**RetLab v1.7.0** is a development fork of DCS Retribution. This is the first pinned build since v1.6.2 (June 2026). It carries 616 changelog entries.

**Download** `retlab-v1.7.0-retlab.zip`, extract it, and run `retribution_main.exe`. The rolling `latest` build is still updated on every change to `main`.

Some campaign changes take effect only in a new game; each changelog entry says so where that applies.

### New campaigns

- Germany: Fulda Gap 1985, Northern Shield 1988
- Afghanistan: Red Bear 1985
- Syria: Anatolian Reach (2004)
- Caucasus: Iron Gate, Abchazian Blitz (from upstream #976)
- Kola: Northern Flank 1985
- Marianas: Second Island Chain (2027)

### Air defense

- Enemy air defenses run on Skynet IADS again; the MOOSE MANTIS bridge is removed.
- A campaign group named `FIXED ...` generates exactly as drawn. Many Syria and Germany campaigns now use real drawn SAM sites.
- SA-2 and SA-3 radars hold fire until the target is well inside range.

### Voice bots on SRS (off by default)

- A SkyEye AWACS replaces the DCS AWACS voice.
- An ABCCC runs player strike packages.
- Vacuum ELINT calls enemy SAM radars by bullseye.
- Voice ATC covers Ground, Tower, Departure and Approach for player flights.
- Take Off can start SRS and SkyEye. Radios can be recorded for Tacview.

### Ground war

- Sector war: the map is split into named sectors, with a battle in every border sector.
- Trains: a Train group drawn in the mission is a rail supply line.
- Supply lines are drawn in the mission file.

### Your flights

- Flight report cards grade each blue flight in the debrief.
- AI fills empty player flights when nobody takes the seats.
- Hand-planned packages arrive 2 to 8 minutes behind their SEAD.
- Player flights get a runway line-up and departure hold.
- Kneeboard bearings are true bearings, and the Mission Info page carries QNH, QFE and wind.
- F-4E: Heatblur stock fits per task, AUX radio presets, and the payload editor clears rails a rack blocks.

### Also

- A damaged ship starts the next mission damaged.
- You can pick targets for your own missile sites.
- An outside AI can read and plan red's turn through a REST API.
- What's New lists recent player-visible changes in the app.
