**RetLab v1.7.1** is a development fork of DCS Retribution. It follows v1.7.0 (November 7, 2026) and carries 56 changelog entries.

**Download** `retlab-v1.7.1-retlab.zip`, extract it, and run `retribution_main.exe`. The rolling `latest` build is still updated on every change to `main`.

Some changes take effect only in a new game; each changelog entry says so where that applies.

### Ground movement (on for new games)

- Supply convoys drive about 120 road miles (60 off-road) a turn and keep their place between turns.
- You can send an armor group to a point, a base or the front.
- Reserves from base stock can join the front.
- You can move a SAM battery; some batteries defend themselves on the road.
- Red's ground moves come only through the outside AI.

### Campaigns

- New: Persian Gulf, Eastern Reach 2025.
- Operation Gazelle is corrected for 1973: Egyptian canal airbases, period types, squadron roles, and runway guns moved.
- 1968 Yankee Station is re-laid at about 1.5 times the scale.
### Your flights

- Saved loadouts are back, in RetLab's own file. One can be a blue squadron's fit for a task.
- The package window has a Fill support button that adds the escort and SEAD the route needs.
- Flights route around known SAM rings (new games).
- AI CAPs refuel on station.
- Flights into darkness carry night-vision goggles.
- The fuel plan uses measured F-4E burn rates, ground time and holds.
- F-16 and F/A-18 radio presets follow the order of a sortie.

### Aircraft and squadrons

- F-15EX V3 mod support: the F-15EX (CFTs) and the F-15IA, in three more modern blue factions.
- Squadron presets for the real units in stock DCS liveries.

### Mission scripts

- MIST no longer loads. CTLD runs on MOOSE's Ops.CTLD and the JTAC drones lase through MOOSE AUTOLASE.
- The bundled MOOSE is develop from November 8, 2026. QRA runs on MOOSE's EASYGCICAP.
- Missions leave out plugins with nothing to do. Five plugins nobody switched on are removed.
- Civilian air traffic is a steady stream of real civil types, clear of the war.

### Fixes

- QRA intercepts work again; the MOOSE update had removed their dispatcher.
- Smart threat reaction follows late locks and retargets.
- A thunderstorm holds front-line CAS.
- A player escort's route ends at its Escort Hold.
- CSAR: unrescued pilots are captured only near an enemy base.
- Vietnam Ops: Arc Light, Super Gaggle and flak fixes; the A-4E CAS fit carries Mk-82 Snakeyes.
- A turn no longer fails on an ambient convoy with no path.
